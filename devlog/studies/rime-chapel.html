The Rime Chapel

Saint Rime, the Frost Chandelier from realm 3 (the one that skitters across the ceiling and down the walls), in a miniature. A frozen chapel sits on a floating rock under an aurora, with a cloister walk round all four sides and open rafters overhead for it to hang from. The camera is close, looking at one side almost face on, and the track runs right round the building. When the fight goes round a corner, the whole miniature turns a quarter with it, the way Fez turns its world. Some attacks are dodged by ducking round the corner. Everything is drawn by code.

Saint Rime, the Frost Chandelier

idle
  • The miniature, up close. The same isometric pixel-art renderer as the bathhouse, but the camera is in close, so you see one side of the chapel at a time: the stone wall with its stained-glass lancet, the walk, the rafters, the lanterns, the snow-heavy roof. The far mountains, aurora and floating rocks are painted flat behind and slide as it turns, so there is always something out there.
  • Round and round. The track is a loop round the chapel, four sides long. When the fight goes round a corner, the miniature turns a quarter under the camera, so the side you are on always faces you and forward is always to the right. The columns of whichever side is nearest the camera are cut away, dollhouse-style; the rafters stay, because it hangs from them.
  • Dodge round the corner. The frost breath shoots straight down one side of the building and out past the corner into the air. Its whole line is dashed first. The answer is to run back round the corner, out of the line, and wait for it to finish. A wall at a corner is cover.
  • Following it round. It crawls the rafters round corners too, and Elfy has to follow. Its spike waves run along the track, so they can come round a corner at you.
  • Its moves (from realm 3): the crawl that grows icicles along the rafters (their shadows sharpen on the walk before they snap); the frost breath; the drop with a wave of ice spikes, then the wreck you hit; and in phase two, the pendulum swing on a chain (crouch), the snap, the crash. Its mask ticks round to a new face before each one.
  • The game is still 2D. The inset shows the loop unrolled into one long line, with the corners dashed. Elfy (green) and the chandelier (red, up at rafter height) move along it, and that is all the game knows about.

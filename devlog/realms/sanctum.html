Realm 5: The Sanctum Above the Clouds

Poison and dark magic in a holy place. A cathedral of white marble and gold leaf afloat on a sea of cloud at sunrise: colonnades on cloud banks, a great rose window, golden balance scales and bells on chains. Something has got into it. Violet-black rot creeps across the marble from the right, the clouds there curdle into tar, the gold tarnishes, and the feathers drifting down are white on the holy side and black on the other. Poison is magenta, dark magic black with violet rims, warnings white for blades and magenta for poison, and the biggest hits land on a gold-on-black impact frame. Drawn at the game's pixel scale, with Elfy for size. Realm 1 · Realm 2 · Realm 3 · Realm 4.

1 · Iaido, the Scales, Flurry and the X · a duelist half-eaten by rot

Ser Aurelian, the Half-Gilt Duelist

A holy fencer, still beautiful down one side: polished gold plate and a white plume. The other side has gone violet-black and wet, and the rapier in that hand drips magenta venom that eats holes in the marble. He fights like a duelist out of a tournament arc: he sheathes, goes still, and is already past you; the cut arrives after the click of the scabbard. He fights round the great balance scales of the Sanctum.

idle
  • Iaido. Warning: he slides the rapier home, sinks low, hand on the hilt, his visor glaring white; the line of the cut is marked across the floor, focus lines close in, and everything goes still, even the falling feathers hang in the air. Get off the floor: Elfy jumps onto a pan of the scales. Then one black frame, and he is on the other side of the room, frozen in his follow-through. Only when he straightens and the scabbard clicks does the cut open behind him, a long gold streak splitting into crescents, sparks off the scales' post, every feather in its path cut in two.
  • The scales. He leaps onto the other pan. His weight drops it and throws her up off hers, high into the air, while he crouches on the pan below, his lunge marked up to her. He thrusts: a glint at the point, a bundle of streaks, an after-image. She flips over it onto the ledge. With his weight gone the scales rock back and settle.
  • Flurry. He follows her onto the ledge and the rapier becomes a storm of little crescents, every one flicking venom; the drops eat smoking pits with glowing magenta rims into the marble, and they stay.
  • The X. Warning: he raises the blade high, an X marked over her. The cut: two great strokes crossing on her, a comic impact star, and she's thrown off the ledge, poisoned, magenta veins climbing her.
  • Weak window. The rot isn't his: his arm seizes and fights him, rot tendrils writhing out of the shoulder, and the sword slips and sticks in the ledge. He kneels clutching the arm while the gold side burns against it. Then he masters it, takes up the sword, and leaps back to his mark.
2 · Gas Net, Popper, Sickle Swarm · three working together

The Plague Cherubs

Three fat little cherubs on moth-eaten wings, faces hidden behind cracked porcelain gas masks with round lenses glowing magenta, each trailing a hose to a tarnished censer that never stops smoking, each with a tiny sickle. They work as a team and they think it's hilarious. The clouds they gas curdle into tar and sink, so the floor of the fight keeps going away.

idle
  • Gas net. Warning: they fly to the corners of a triangle round her and the lines between them are marked. Then a sheet of magenta gas is stretched between them, swirling, and the triangle closes. Elfy jumps out to the next cloud. The net collapses onto the cloud she left: it curdles to tar from the top down, drips, and sinks to the floor, where it lies as a bubbling pool.
  • Popper. One flies in close and swells up like a puffball, trembling, lenses wide, gas leaking from it; its blast is ringed. It pops: a white flash, a burst of lumpy poison cloud, and it shoots off spinning like a let-go balloon. Her cloud curdles under her.
  • Sickle swarm. They line up overhead, bouncing with giggles; three dive lines are marked, one after another. Each rears up and dives, a ring of blade spinning round it, cutting feathers; Elfy runs under them, the first two miss, the third catches her: a comic impact star, an impact frame, poisoned.
  • Weak window. They bounce off the floor into each other and land in a heap, masks askew, lenses spinning, stars circling, burping little puffs of gas. Then they flutter up and home, and the clouds grow back in a glitter of gold.
3 · Toll, Grave Bell · a set piece that hunts you

The Tolling Hollow

A great cracked bronze bell, green with verdigris, hanging from a cloud gone to rot. Its clapper is one bloodshot violet eye on a tendon, and it watches you through the cracks. The smaller bells hanging round it are the only footing left once the glass is gone, and every toll sets them swinging.

idle
  • Toll. Warning: it rears back, slowly, its eye burning through the cracks; rings of sound are marked spreading from it and the glass ledges flicker. Elfy jumps onto a small bell. Then it swings and the eye-clapper strikes the rim: a comic star on the bronze, a gold-and-black impact frame, three rings of sound that shatter both ledges into tumbling shards of coloured glass. Every bell in the room swings with it, Elfy riding hers.
  • Tar. It rocks, settling, tar dripping off its lip and hissing in little pools on the marble.
  • Grave bell. Warning: the chain snaps link by link, sparks at each; its outline is marked on the floor round her, focus lines closing in. It drops: a slam that throws the clouds aside, an impact frame. Then it tolls three times with her inside, jumping on the floor, light blasting out through its cracks.
  • Weak window. Black tendrils reach down from the rot cloud, wrap the crown and haul it back up. It rang itself dizzy: it hangs there wobbling, stars round its crown, its eye lolled out of its mouth on its tendon, spinning and drooling tar. Elfy staggers out, seeing stars too. Then the glass flies back up off the floor into the ledges.
4 · Hex Sigil, Grasping Hands, Uppercut, Hammer Smash, the Chain · a puppeteer and its puppet

Acolyte Nil and the Stitched Seraph

A tiny hooded child-priest with a candle for a staff and two pale eyes in the hood, and a hulking angel stitched together out of broken statues with violet thread: a ribcage for a chest with a violet heart beating in it, a serene cracked face, one stone wing, one great gilded fist off some other saint. Nil works it like a marionette from a high ledge, writes hex sigils in the air with the candle, and calls hands up out of the floor. Hit Nil and the threads snap.

idle
  • A puppet. At rest the Seraph hangs slack on its threads, head lolling, fists swinging. When Nil takes up the threads it straightens. Every move it makes, Nil's hands make first.
  • Hex sigil and the hands. Warning: Nil writes in the air with the candle, the pen point sparking, and the sigil burns itself into the floor round her, ring, star, runes, until it flares white. She jumps, but a pool opens and shadow arms burst out of it, catch her and drag her down, clenched round her. She's cursed: a dark stain climbing her, violet smoke, a glitching skull over her head.
  • Uppercut. The Seraph stomps over and crouches, its gilded fist low behind it; the blow is marked up through her, focus lines closing in. The hands let go as it hits: a comic impact star, a gold-on-black impact frame, and she's launched, flipping onto the ledge.
  • Hammer smash and the chain. Both fists go up overhead, slowly, a dome marked over the ledge; she goes up onto a cloud and the fists come down: a dome of force, the end of the ledge broken off, rubble bouncing, the marble cracked. Nil writes another sigil; a chain shoots out, locks onto her cloud and yanks it out from under her.
  • Weak window. She drops, slides between the Seraph's legs, leaps onto Nil's ledge and dashes straight through Nil. The threads snap and whip away, and the Seraph falls apart: head, cage, wing and limbs tumbling into a heap of statue. Nil sits dazed, hood knocked back, candle out. Then Nil relights it, the threads shoot out, and the pieces fly back together across the room.
5 · Black Halo, Molt, Eclipse, False Smite · corrupted holy magic

The Molting Saint

A weeping angel statue floating before the rose window, hands over her face. Her stone wings are molting, feathers gone loose and some gone black, and her halo has eclipsed into a black ring with a pearl corona. A column of light beside her lifts Elfy up to the ledges, while it lasts.

idle
  • Black halo. Warning: she uncovers her face, blank sockets streaked with rot, and lifts the halo off her head, arching back; its path is marked as a great loop round the room. She throws it: spinning, trailing, it scythes round at shin height, cutting every feather it passes, and comes back to her hand. Elfy jumps it.
  • Molt. She folds her wings tight round herself, focus lines closing in, then bursts them open with an impact frame. Stone feathers fly off, brake and rock in the air, then turn to point, lines marked to where they'll strike, and stab down together as darts. They root in the marble as violet thorns, and stay. Elfy rides the column of light up to the ledge.
  • Eclipse. A black moon slides across the rose window, a pearl corona round it, and the light goes out of the room. The column of light turns into a downdraft.
  • False smite. A glint marks her on the ledge and a dotted line falls from the sky. She drops off the ledge, into the downdraft, and it slams her down into the thorns, cursed. Then a bolt of black light splits the ledge where she stood: black core, white rim, an inverted star, cracks glowing violet.
  • Weak window. The eclipse passes and she sinks, wings drooping, hands to her face again, and weeps real light: tears of gold running between her fingers and falling, her halo flickering gold. Then the thorns crumble.
6 · Tendrils, Soul Drain, Necro Skulls, the Shatter, the Burst · the boss of the Sanctum

The Eclipse Heart

The source of the rot. Where the rose window broke, a black sphere hangs in a tilted banded disk of light, debris spiralling into it, a pale rim round it and a pearl corona, and in front of it floats a cracked marble mask, serene, its eyeholes glowing violet. Halfway through, the mask shatters and it powers up.

idle
  • Tendrils. Bladed tendrils coil out of it, barbed, a white-edged crescent at each tip; the line of each lash is marked across a storey. The first lashes along the floor: the loop runs down the length of the whip and the tip cracks with a white pop and a ring of air. Elfy goes up onto a cloud. The second lashes at cloud height, and she drops under it.
  • Soul drain. A funnel is outlined to her, then it drinks: pale wisps stream out of her, spiralling down its throat. She runs, and slides toward it anyway, until she dashes free and the funnel snaps.
  • Necro skulls. Four spectral skulls circle it, smoke trailing, eyes lighting one by one; each one's landing spot is ringed. They fire one after another: she runs, jumps, runs again, and the fourth catches her on the cloud. Cursed.
  • The shatter. The mask trembles, light leaking from its cracks, focus lines closing in, the room darkening. Then it shatters: an impact frame, a violet aura flaring off the bottom of the whole screen and in spikes round the sphere, the corona streaming huge, and both clouds torn loose and pulled into orbit round it.
  • The burst and the weak window. The core pulses and contracts. Then it bursts: a black star across a violet-white flash, three shockwaves, the clouds flung home, Elfy blown back. It hangs collapsed, small, smoking, its white core showing through the cracks, until the shards of the mask fly back together.

Psychic Triple Jump in 3D

The first triple jump, rebuilt on the 3D body so every drawing is a real pose, now with her face. She crouches, jumps, blasts the air under her boots for a second jump, blasts harder and somersaults on the third, then lands in a squash. The face cam follows her head so you can read every expression.

Face cam

  • Crouch: eyes narrow, focused.
  • Rise: a smile, arms thrown up.
  • Each blast: fierce, teeth showing, eyes glowing green, and a green aura outlines her.
  • Somersault: a real forward flip; her face hides while she is upside down.
  • Landing: a squash, then a pleased smile.

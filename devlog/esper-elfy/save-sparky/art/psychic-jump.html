Psychic Triple Jump

One rig, five heights. She jumps, blasts the air beneath her for a second jump, blasts again and flips for a third, then lands in a squash. The undercut, pointed ears, green eyes, cropped purple hooded jacket, black bodysuit, fingerless gloves and green psychic fire follow the reference. Boots are purple, as asked. Her face changes with the move: focused in the crouch, a smile on the rise, fierce with glowing eyes on each blast, and a smile on landing.

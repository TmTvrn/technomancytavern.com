Save Sparky · 48 px animation

Kick, Spin, Victory in 3D

Every frame is drawn from a posed 3D body, then turned into 48 px pixel art with hard shading steps and outlines. She pivots into a side kick with her head turned to the target, turns a real full circle, and lands the victory pose. The hand-placed face rides on top, narrowing as her head turns. The animation runs at 15 drawings a second, like hand-animated sprites; the fire and sparks move at full speed.

Turnaround

The same body at eight facings, 45° apart. This is what makes a true spin possible: every angle is drawn, none are squashed.

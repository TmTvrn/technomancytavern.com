Sparky at Four Resolutions

One simulation, drawn four times. Sparky hops through the blizzard with squash and stretch while corrupted skulls circle him. Every shape is math, so each resolution draws the same scene with as much detail as its pixels allow.

  • The motion is identical in all four. Anticipation crouch, stretch on takeoff, a hang at the top, squash on landing with a springy overshoot, and an ear and tail that lag behind.
  • Watch what drops out at low resolution: the skulls' teeth and the glint in Sparky's eye are thinner than one pixel at the small sizes, so they only appear in the larger views. At 160 the skulls are blobs with eye sockets.
  • Outlines stay one pixel at every size, so they look bold at 160 and fine at 640.
  • Slow motion shows the squash steps. At 160 the squash snaps between a few sizes. At 640 it is nearly smooth.

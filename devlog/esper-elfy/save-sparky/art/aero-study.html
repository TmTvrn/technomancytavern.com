How the hybrid is drawn

Every frame, the hybrid paints into two small 320 × 180 grids. Shapes write colors into the color grid. Anything that shines also adds light into the light grid. At the end, the color grid is scaled up with sharp pixels, and the light grid is scaled up smooth and added on top.

What makes it a hybrid

  • Pure pixel art keeps everything on the grid, light included, usually with a small fixed palette.
  • pixelfx is nearly that: it also has a separate light grid, but blends it in at pixel size, so glow is blocky too.
  • This hybrid changes one step: the light grid is enlarged smoothly before it is added. That single change brings back the soft HDR glow Save Sparky already has.
  • The rest is Save Sparky: shapes drawn by code (no sprites), squash and stretch rounded to whole pixels, and colors that each mean one thing.

Orbiting orbs

This is how the necro skulls work, applied to psychic orbs around the hero. Each orb moves on a flat circle seen from slightly above. On screen that circle becomes a thin ellipse. The orb's depth decides two things: whether it is painted before the hero (behind) or after (in front), and how dim it is.

angle a = time × speed + orb_index × (360° ÷ count) screen x = cos(a) × 34 the circle's full width screen y = sin(a) × 9 squashed: we look at it from slightly above depth z = sin(a) −1 far side … +1 near side z < 0 → paint before the hero, one color step darker z ≥ 0 → paint after the hero, full brightness

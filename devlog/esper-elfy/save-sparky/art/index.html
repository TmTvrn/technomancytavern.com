◆ ART STUDIES ◆

How she got drawn

Live experiments, in the order the art grew: one simulation drawn two ways, skulls, six ways to draw Elfy, the triple jump, and two attempts at 3D. Everything is drawn by code in your browser.

SAME SIMULATION, TWO DRAWINGS

Blizzard Study

One snowstorm in the arena, run by a single simulation and drawn two ways. Same flakes, same gusts, same boss shot, frame for frame. Only the drawing changes.

Aero Study

The same arena on a bright Frutiger Aero morning, drawn two ways from one simulation, with a live breakdown of how the hybrid builds each frame.

Sparky at Four Resolutions

One simulation, drawn four times. Sparky hops through the blizzard with squash and stretch while corrupted skulls circle him.

SKULLS AND DETAIL

Laughing Skull

The 48 px skull bursts out laughing: the jaw drops in whole pixels, the head bobs, the eyes flare. Six smaller skulls circle it.

Skull Detail Ladder

One skull at eight sprite sizes. Each step up adds features drawn in whole pixels, the way a pixel artist would.

FINDING ELFY

Six Ways to Draw Her

The same psychic heroine at 48 pixels tall, drawn six ways. Pick an expression, or let them cycle.

Heroic at Four Sizes

The heroic style at 48, 64, 80 and 96 pixels tall. Each size up gives her face more pixels, and the face rules use them.

Heroic Face Pass

The 48 px heroine with a hand-placed face, with a Before toggle to compare against the last version.

THE TRIPLE JUMP AND THE KICK

Psychic Triple Jump (first version)

The first triple jump, before the face pass. One rig, five heights.

Psychic Triple Jump (with the face pass)

One rig, five heights. She jumps, blasts the air beneath her for a second jump, blasts again and flips for a third, then lands in a squash.

Kick, Spin, Victory

A side kick with a hit-stop freeze and afterimages, then a full spin and a V-sign victory pose.

THREE DIMENSIONS, DRAWN BY HAND

Kick, Spin, Victory in 3D

Every frame drawn from a posed 3D body, then turned into 48 px pixel art with hard shading steps and outlines.

Psychic Triple Jump in 3D

The triple jump rebuilt on the 3D body, so every drawing is a real pose.

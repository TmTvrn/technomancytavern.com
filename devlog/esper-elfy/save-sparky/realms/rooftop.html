Realm 10: The Psi-Tower Rooftop

The roof of a skyscraper academy at sunset, where the city's psychic kids come to settle things. A striped retro sun sinking behind a neon skyline, a water tower, a VS billboard, a painted helipad; and three slabs of the roof torn up and held in the air by somebody's telekinesis, bobbing in a violet glow. These eight are Elfy's size, which makes them fast: shonen psychics with chunky boots, big shiny eyes and a pose for everything, who talk while they fight. Their words pop up over their heads when they laugh or leer, along with the menace marks, anger veins and sweat drops. Every attack still shows itself first (a dashed outline is a warning), but the windows are short. Realm 9: the Midas night bazaar.

1 · the Blink, the Afterimages, the Time Out · teleport, speed

Zip Zephyr

A teleporting speedster: spiky navy hair, cyan goggles pushed up, a white racing suit, red boots and a red scarf twice as long as she is. She blinks. A dashed outline of her appears where she is about to be, and a beat later she is there, kicking. She is also, for all that, not very fit.

idle
  • The blink. ZIP: she is gone in a spray of sparks, and her dashed outline appears where she is going. TOO SLOW! Beside you, in a flying kick (jump over her). UP HERE! Above you, a dive kick into the spot (dash away). BEHIND YOU~ A high kick through the air (crouch). Each outline gets about a third of a second.
  • The point. She lands, points, and says HMPH! AMATEUR., menace marks drifting up beside her.
  • The scarf boomerang. She blinks to the far side: OVER HERE~ CATCH! Its whole flight is dashed while she winds up: out at head height (crouch), back low along the floor (jump). After the boomerang-throwing bosses of the research notes.
  • The wind-blade ring. She blinks up into the air and throws out a ring of eight blades, every ray dashed first: stand in a gap. The second ring is turned half a step, so a blade comes right down your gap: jump into the next one. After the reappear-and-ring pattern.
  • Afterimages (phase two). She splits into smears that rip across the whole roof down lanes that flicker first, each with an arrow for its direction: low (jump), mid (crouch), then both at once (get up on a slab).
  • Time out, the weak window. She blinks back in, hands on her knees: HAH... HAH... Then she sits down on the helipad, tongue out, sweating. TIME OUT... Elfy hits her three times. NO FAIR!! and she blinks home.
2 · Pellets, the Charged Shot, the Wall Kick, the Giga Buster · electrokinesis, an arm cannon

Gigawatt Gail

An arm-cannon kid: an orange helmet with a lightning-bolt fin, a magenta bob, gold eyes, a fang, a navy suit and a buster where her right hand should be. Pellets, a charged shot, a wall kick, and one shot she charges for far too long.

idle
  • Pellets. READY? GO! PEW PEW PEW: three quick shots straight down a lane at head height that flickers first. Get up on a slab.
  • The charged shot. KIIIN: sparks spiral into her muzzle, she squats into it, brows down (EAT THIS!), and her aim line is drawn to the slab you are standing on. Drop off it before the BOOM.
  • The wall kick. She dashes to the wall, kicks off it onto the far slab and scatters three shots onto rings on the floor. Stand in the gap.
  • The staggered volley. TRY THIS! She drops to the floor and fires three shots at three heights, one at a time, each lane flickering first: low (jump), high (crouch), low (jump). After the staggered belly-spike volleys in the research.
  • The bounce shots. BOUNCY BOUNCY! Each shot's hops are dashed first: one in big hops that sail just over your head (crouch), then one in little hops along the floor (jump). After the rebound-stone pattern.
  • The giga buster (phase two). FULL POWER!! HEH HEH HEH... She charges for a long two seconds, trembling, grit lifting off the roof, menace marks rising, and the beam's whole box is outlined: everything below the middle slab. Climb to the top. GIGA BUSTER!! The beam fills the roof and the recoil drives her back to the wall.
  • PFFT, the weak window. The cannon backfires. Sooty, hair blown into an afro, coughing smoke: ...COUGH. Elfy hits her three times. OKAY OKAY! THAT WAS A WARM-UP!
3 · the Combo, the Plunge, the Rewind, Too Far · chronokinesis, a saber

Ronin Rewind

A saber kid who can rewind time: a long violet-black ponytail held with a gold clock hairpin, a white haori with pink trim over plum hakama, and a blade of pink light. Stoic, few words. She does her combo; then she rewinds the whole roof, Elfy included, and does it again faster. Then she rewinds once more, much too far.

idle
  • The combo. Hand on the hilt, menace rising: ... Then ZAN! A dash down a flickering lane that stops just short, a low crescent along the floor (jump), a high crescent (crouch), a rising slash, SHING! Every crescent's lane flickers first.
  • The plunge. She leaps to the high slab, blade point-down; a line is drawn from her to your spot. DON! She plunges into it (dash away), and a shockwave runs out both ways (jump). TOO SLOW.
  • The rewind (phase two). << REWIND: a clock face spins backwards behind the roof, tracking lines roll across the picture, and everything runs back to the start, Elfy included. Then the same combo again at one and a half times the speed, with a pink ghost of her first pass a beat behind her. AGAIN?
  • The counter. ...COME. Eyes shut, a pink ring tightening round her, then she cuts eight ways, every ray dashed first: stand in a gap. The second burst is turned half a step: jump into the next one. After the guard-and-counter-burst pattern.
  • The slide slash. HAA! From a crouch, because you are far away, she slides the whole floor at you (double jump), then flips back over you. ...NOT BAD. After the crouch-then-slide decision in the research.
  • Too far, the weak window. ONE MORE TIME! She rewinds again, and it's this morning: pajamas, nightcap, pillow, fast asleep standing up. ...ZZZ. FIVE MORE MINUTES... Elfy hits her three times. She wakes: !? D-DON'T LOOK!! FAST-FORWARD >>, and back to now.
4 · the Wall, the Box, the Lasso, the Mirror, the Box Again · a mime whose mimes are real

Copycat Marcel

A psychic mime: white face, black teardrop marks, a beret, a striped shirt, white gloves. Whatever he mimes is real. While he is miming it, it shows as a dashed outline (the warning); when it becomes real it turns to shimmering glass. He never says a word, only punctuation.

idle
  • The wall. He closes his eyes and feels for it with flat palms; a dashed wall appears in front of him, an arrow beside it. Then SHOVE: it turns to glass and slides the whole length of the roof. Be up on a slab.
  • The box. ! He strains under a heavy box over his head (dashed), and the slab you are standing on is marked. He throws it, THUNK. Be off it.
  • The lasso. He twirls it, throws it, YOINK: it loops round Elfy and reels her in toward his outlined punch. HO HO~. Jump the moment the rope goes taut: TWANG, it snaps, and she double-jumps right over him. WHIFF. ?!
  • The invisible man. SHHH... He fades out, all but his white gloves, which give him away: each lunge is dashed out from them first. Along the floor (get up on a slab), an uppercut onto your slab (get down), back along the floor (double jump). After the camouflage-and-hidden-lunge pattern.
  • The balloon animals. He blows up four balloons, SQUEAK, and they drift after Elfy; the ring of each pop is outlined before POP POP POP! Outrun them. After the bubble-burst pattern.
  • The mirror (phase two). He backflips away and a pane of glass stands between them; on the other side of it he copies Elfy exactly, step for step, jump for jump. Then he punches it out, CRASH, and its shards land on ringed spots on her side. Stand in the gap.
  • The box again, the weak window. ?!?! He mimes being trapped in a box, and the box is real. Elfy hits it three times; the third, SHATTER! Dizzy, he bows deeply (MERCI.) and moonwalks home.
5 · the Cheer, the Cartwheel, the Pyramid, the Victory Twirl · pyrokinesis, school spirit

Pom-Pom Pyra

A pyrokinetic cheerleader: a red and gold uniform with a big P, a white pleated skirt, a high ponytail that burns at the tip, and pom-poms that are balls of fire. Her cheers are her attacks: GIMME A G! and a giant burning G drops on you. Relentlessly peppy.

idle
  • The cheer. READY? OKAY! GIMME A G! She throws her pom-poms up, and a giant G is outlined right where you stand; a beat later it falls on that spot, burning, FWOOM. GIMME AN O! Then WHAT'S THAT SPELL?! and the exclamation mark. Three spots, three dodges, each letter burning a while after it lands.
  • The cartwheel. The lane flickers; CARTWHEEL! She wheels the length of the roof, leaving a trail of fire along the floor, and bounces off the wall, BOING, up onto a slab. TA-DAA! Be high.
  • Spirit fingers. SPIRIT FINGERS! Six arms of fire wheel out of her jazz hands, each set dashed first. The first set misses you; the second is turned onto exactly where you stood, so drop to the floor between its arms. After the rotating spiral-burst pattern.
  • The spirit chain. SPREAD THE SPIRIT!! The floor flickers, she slams her pom-poms down, FWOOOOM, and columns of fire march wall to wall one after another. Be up on a slab. WOO! GO TEAM! After the chain of flame pillars in the research.
  • Pyramid formation (phase two). Six fire pom-poms stack into a pyramid over her head, and she launches them one by one onto rings where Elfy just was: the high slab, then the floor, then the floor further on. Keep moving, and climb onto the far slab.
  • The victory twirl, the weak window. AND A ONE, AND A TWO... she twirls, and twirls. WHEEE... Dizzy, she topples off her slab, PLOP, and her flames go out, FSSS. Elfy hits her three times. GO, ELFY! ...WAIT, NO!
6 · the Skate, the Curtain, the Rink, the Triple Axel, the Sneeze · cryokinesis, too cool

Frostbyte

A cryokinetic too-cool kid: an ice-blue swoop of hair over one eye, big headphones, a navy hoodie with a snowflake on it, and his hands in his pockets the whole fight. Lids half down, one-word lines. He skates. He does not, it turns out, wear a scarf.

idle
  • The skate. YAWN. ...CHILL. He snaps his fingers, a lane of floor flickers, and he skates the length of it, hands still in his pockets. TCH. Spikes of ice burst up along his trail (outlined first). Be up on a slab.
  • The curtain. LET IT SNOW. He points one finger up, and every column of the roof is outlined but one, its spot ringed. Icicles fall down all the others. Find the gap. AGAIN.: a second curtain with the gap somewhere else.
  • The ice statues. STAY FROSTY. He breathes two blocks of ice into being, his smirk frozen in each, then skates up and kicks them along the floor, KIK!, each lane dashed first. Jump, jump. After the ice-statue pattern.
  • The blizzard. BRRR-ING IT ON. Spikes grow along the far wall (outlined first) and a gale shoves Elfy toward them; keep running against it until it drops. ...MEH. After the blizzard and wing-gust patterns.
  • The rink (phase two). ...FINE. Headphones on, head nodding, TSS TSS, and the whole roof freezes. Get up on a slab.
  • Single, double, TRIPLE! He skates and spins into an axel that lands on the slab you are on (its ring outlined), bursting into spikes: left, high, right. Hop ahead, slab to slab, and drop to the floor for the last one.
  • The sneeze, the weak window. ...FLAWLESS. AH... AH... ACHOO!! He sneezes himself solid into a block of ice, eyes wide. Elfy jumps up and hits the block three times; the third, SHATTER! B-B-BRR... ...WHATEVER.
7 · the Slab Slam, the Well, Upsy-Daisy, the Heavy Drop · gravity, density

Anchor Annie

A gravity kid, small and unimaginably heavy: twin buns, a sailor collar with a gold anchor on it, a red bow, and gauntlets as big as her head. She pulls the floating slabs down out of the air, opens wells that drag you in, throws pieces of the roof up and lets them fall. Very touchy about her size.

idle
  • The slab slam. HMPH! GET DOWN HERE! She reaches up, and the floor under a floating slab is outlined: the slab comes down onto it, THOOM, sits a moment, and floats back up. Then the middle one. Get out from under.
  • The well. SQUISH TIME~ A sphere is outlined on the floor, then it is a black hole with a violet rim, and everything on the floor streams toward it. Elfy runs and still slides. Jump up onto a slab before it bursts, KA-WHUMP.
  • The orbit. ROUND AND ROUND! Chunks of roof wheel round her on a ring that keeps growing outward; when it reaches your slab, be in a gap. After the expanding-ring pattern.
  • The cannonball. ANCHORS AWEIGH!! She curls into a ball and her whole route is dashed: along the floor, off the wall, past your slab (jump), off the sky, off the far wall and home. After the jump-and-roll and bouncing-orb patterns.
  • Upsy-daisy (phase two). FWUMP: four chunks of roof lift out of the floor into the sky and hang there, each over an outlined ring. Get down between them before she drops them all at once, CRASH!
  • The heavy drop. HEAVY DROP!! She leaps clean out of the picture. A ring follows Elfy across the floor, then locks, flashing white. Dash.
  • Dense, the weak window. KRAKOOM!! She made herself so heavy she went straight through the roof to her waist. ...UH OH. Elfy hits her three times (MY BUNS!). POP: I'M NOT SHORT! I'M DENSE!!
8 · Telekinesis, the Mindlings, the Ultimate Brain Beam, One Thought Too Many · the final act

The Magnificent Cerebrax

The one holding up the slabs: a small boy with an enormous pink brain under a glass dome, a monocle, a violet cape with its collar up to his ears, riding a hover-saucer. He monologues. He throws the roof at you, sends little brains after you, fires the ultimate brain beam, and then thinks one thought too many.

idle
  • Telekinesis. BEHOLD!! MWAHAHAHA! His brain glows and he tears the middle slab out of the air, shaking, lowers it to a lane that flickers along the floor, and hurls it spinning: jump. Then the right slab, down a lane at head height: crouch. FOOLISH ELF! They float back into place.
  • The synapse curtain. SYNAPSE! Two orbs part to the walls and a bolt joins them, its line dashed first: low (get up on a slab), then high (get back down). After the electric-curtain pattern.
  • The brainstorm. A cloud of thoughts rains down every dashed column: stand between two, then shift half a step for the second wave. After the ceiling-rain pattern.
  • The mindlings (phase two). MINDLINGS, ATTACK! Three little winged brains pop out of his dome and circle. One at a time, each locks a dashed line onto where Elfy is standing and dives, SPLAT, leaving a puddle a moment. Dash, dash, dash.
  • The ultimate brain beam (phase three). ULTIMATE... BRAIN... BEAM!!! It charges with menace marks rising, its path and the whole floor outlined; then the beam's foot sweeps the floor from one wall to the other in sparks. Get up on a slab.
  • One thought too many, the weak window. ...WAIT. IF I'M SO SMART... ERROR!? The dome fogs, the saucer dies, and he drops to the roof, CLONK, dizzy and sparking. Elfy hits the dome three times; the third, CRACK! MY BEAUTIFUL BRAIN! THIS ISN'T OVER!!

Realm 3: The Hell Cavern

Ice in hell. A basalt cavern under the world: lava falls pouring off ledges into a river glowing behind the floor, stalactites, and against all that red a second colour, ultraviolet soulfire in iron braziers and violet crystal veins in the rock. Embers rise from where the heat is, ride the updrafts, pool on the ceiling and fall as ash; ice snuffs them, and frost turns lava to black glass that thaws again. Drawn at the game's pixel scale, with Elfy for size. Realm 1 · Realm 2.

1 · Nova, Breath, Whack · a triple combo

The Icebox Family

Three who fight as one. Papa Fridge, a hulking rounded refrigerator on clawed casters in a maroon bathrobe, his face a set of fridge magnets that rearrange themselves with his mood and his freezer door for a mouth. Grandma Freezerburn, a frost ghost in a lavender shawl and pearls, knitting needles in her ghostly hands, her head an ice crystal turning in its own cold light with a face of cracks. Cubey, a tiny ice-cube-tray kid on stubby legs, each cube one pixel of his face, with a mallet.

idle
  • 1 · Grandma's frost nova. Warning: she floats over and winds up, rising and stretching, her crystal spinning faster, frost spiralling in, her shawl flaring, a ring on the floor showing how far it will reach. Then a white flash, a snowflake, and three rings of ice crystals bloom out. Every ember they cross dies in a hiss of steam and falls as ash. Elfy is chilled: frost ferns creep up her and she can only crawl.
  • 2 · Papa's frost breath. Warning: he rolls in on his casters, then rears back and swells up with his door clamped shut, his magnets bunching into a scowl, the cone of the breath outlined. Then the freezer door flies open on icicle teeth and he blasts: billowing frost, wind streaks, tumbling crystals. Frost races up Elfy and a faceted block crystallises round her pane by pane, a glint sweeping across it. The lava fall behind them freezes to black glass with glowing cracks and thaws back from the top later.
  • 3 · Cubey's whack. He runs in on his stubby legs, goes up on his toes and winds the mallet way back (the biggest anticipation in the fight). Cracks race over the block; then the swing, a white flash, and the block bursts into chunks that tumble, bounce and skid while Elfy flies free, landing on one knee, still frosted.
  • The family. Papa laughs with his door flapping and his letter magnets bouncing, Grandma spins, Cubey hops round with a grin made of cubes; then they go home.
  • The cavern. Embers born at the foot of each fall, in pops on the river and in violet sparks off the soulfire, climbing the updrafts and pulled in toward them, pooling on the ceiling and coming down as ash; cinders that tumble and crack in two; a burning fleck now and then; big soft embers out of focus in front of everything; the air over the river shimmering; and every few seconds the whole cavern swells, a heartbeat.
  • The full bout. In phase two Papa charges: he rears back, casters spitting sparks, the lane to the wall lit, and rolls the length of the room (double jump over him). He hits the wall and goes over. Weak window: flat on his back, door flapping, while Cubey hops about behind him. He rights himself and rolls home (hop him again).
2 · Icicles, Breath and the Fall · it walks on the ceiling

Saint Rime, the Frost Chandelier

A cathedral chandelier that became a predator. A cage of black iron rimed with frost, carried upside-down across the ceiling and down the walls on six long jointed legs that end in hooks. Candles burn on its arms with cold blue flame, crystal prisms swing beneath it, and a veil of torn frozen lace hangs round its heart: a drum of porcelain masks, serene, weeping, screaming, sleeping. Before every attack it ticks round to a new face. No ember survives near it.

idle
  • The masks are the warning. The drum ticks round, the porcelain turning edge-on and back, to the face of what's coming: weeping before the icicles, screaming before the breath, sleeping before it drops, then snapping to screaming as it lets go.
  • Icicle fall. It crawls the length of the ceiling on its tripod gait, prisms swinging behind it, and icicles grow in its wake. They drip and tremble while their shadows sharpen on the floor, then snap off their roots in sequence and stab down, bursting into skidding shards.
  • Frost breath. It goes over the corner and down the wall, hanging off it sideways, and draws back against the rock (anticipation) while the mask's mouth opens and fills with white light; the breath's corridor is outlined across the room. Then it screams frost the width of the cavern: every ember in its path dies to steam and ash, and the lava falls freeze to black glass one after another as it reaches them. Crouch under it.
  • The fall. It crosses back to hang over Elfy; its candles flare; a target and the reach of the spikes show on the floor. It lets go, legs folding in as it falls, and crashes: a frost nova, chunks of ice, and a frost line racing out both ways with clusters of ice spikes bursting up behind it, glinting, and shattering. Jump them.
  • Weak window. It lies crumpled on its back, legs twitching, the mask turned up, until a chain drops from the ceiling and it winches itself back up, legs dangling.
  • The full bout. In phase two it crawls to the middle of the ceiling and lowers itself on a chain, the whole arc of the swing dashed out, and swings the room like a pendulum, legs out, candles flaring (stay crouched). The chain snaps, its landing outlined, and it crashes; ice spikes race out both ways (jump). Weak window: crumpled on the floor, the weeping mask, before it winches itself up.
3 · The Seal, the Lances and the Stamp · it climbs and leaps

The Clerk of Rime

A towering, stilt-thin thing in a long black greatcoat with a collar of ice shards, frost dripping off its hem. Its head is a window pane in an iron frame, fogged over; on the inside something draws a face in the frost with a fingertip, wipes it, and draws another. A ledger is chained to one wrist and it carries a heavy iron seal in the other hand. It goes where it pleases: it seals a lava fall solid and climbs it, and leaps between the platforms that hang on chains.

idle
  • The face in the glass. Every time its mood changes a fingertip-wide streak wipes the frost clear, and the new face is drawn back in stroke by stroke: blank, then narrowed, then a glare, then a grin. The window is lit violet from inside.
  • The seal and the ladder. It brings its seal down on a lava fall: a glyph of frost flashes on it and the fall freezes to glass, top to bottom. It climbs it like a ladder, hand over hand, and steps off onto the highest platform.
  • Ice lance. Warning: it leans back, frost gathering into a spear in its long fingers, an aim line to Elfy. Then it throws: a faceted lance, a pulse of light running down its core, rings of cold shearing off the tip, a frost wake. It stabs into the floor and bursts.
  • Leap and stamp. It crouches (anticipation), springs stretched, arcs across with its coat-tails trailing, and lands squashing; it stamps the platform with the seal, a white line runs down to the floor, and a frost line races out along it with clusters of ice spikes bursting up behind. Jump them.
  • The volley. A great leap off the high platform; at the top it hangs with every lance raised while three marks blink on the floor; it throws all three as it falls and lands in a crouch that shakes the room. Then it writes in its ledger: the weak window.
  • The full bout. In phase two it slams the ledger shut and strides at Elfy. The seal goes up overhead, its glyph's landing outlined (dash back), comes down, and the glyph bursts into a pillar of ice. Then it spins, its coat-tails throwing a ring of frost round at shin height (jump). Weak window: it overbalances onto one knee.
4 · Vortex, Burst and Freeze · it floats

The Snowglobe Oracle

A great snowglobe floating over the lava, a little dead village of black spires inside it with a few windows still lit, on a collar of gold clockwork, a fortune-teller's violet shawl hung from it with gold tassels, chains of charms dangling below. It has no face of its own. The snow inside settles into one; when it shakes, the face blows apart into a blizzard and settles again, worse. Every attack cracks its glass a little more, and snow leaks out of the cracks.

idle
  • Its faces. Flakes of snow drift through the dead village and settle into a face: watching, then staring, then raging with teeth, then a grin. Before each attack it shakes and the face blows apart into a swirling blizzard inside the glass, then settles into the next.
  • Blizzard vortex. It shakes, then turns itself slowly upside down (overshooting and settling), the village hanging from the top; its path is marked on the floor. Snow pours out of its lid into a funnel that stands up off the floor, bands of snow spinning in front of it and behind, snow boiling at its foot. Then the funnel comes loose and stalks across the room, dragging at Elfy while she runs against it, every ember dying in it, until it collapses in a burst of snow.
  • Snowflake burst. Frost gathers in a ring round it, closing in; then a cold flash and big six-armed snowflakes spin out, slow, tumble and drift down; where they settle the floor rimes white.
  • The freeze. A grinning face settles, and Elfy's own reflection appears in the glass, small and warped, and frosts over from the feet up while frost creeps across the floor to her. Then it happens to her: frost races up her and she is sealed in a faceted block. She shakes, the block cracks, and she bursts free.
  • Weak window. Spent, it dims and sags toward the floor, and frost slowly seals the cracks in its glass.
  • The full bout. In phase two a raging face: it rises shaking, its landing and its lane outlined, and the glass drops out of its collar and rolls the room like a boulder (double jump over), smashing into the wall. Weak window: cracked, leaking snow, while its empty collar and shawl hang where it was; then it floats back up into them, spinning.
5 · The Overture and the Crescendo · lightning: a conductor and its swarm

Maestro Ohm and the Sparkflies

The lightning begins. A gaunt alien conductor, too tall, in a tattered tailcoat with a violet lining and a stiff high collar. Its head is a brass gramophone horn turned toward you; inside the bell, two cold eyes slide slowly round the rim and a mouth opens at the bottom. Its baton is a lightning rod wound in copper. Out of its coat-tails come the Sparkflies, balls of lightning with crackling wings, and they carry the current wherever it points.

idle
  • The overture. Its coat-tails open and the Sparkflies hatch one after another, drifting out in arcs into a loose net across the room. Warning: it raises the baton high and holds it still, the other hand clawing the air; sparks pour into the tip until it's white, the cavern darkens round it, and faint links flicker from fly to fly. Then it snaps the baton down like a whip and the lightning leaps from the baton through every fly into Elfy, three strokes down the same path, every ember along it flaring white. Elfy is electrified: she flashes, lit through on the brightest flickers, sparks crawling over her, jittering, thrown back.
  • Break the chain. In the game, every fly is a link: kill one and the lightning fizzles at the gap.
  • The crescendo. It conducts faster and faster, both arms whipping, coat-tails flying. The flies leave the net and ring Elfy, orbiting faster, tightening, crackling to one another. Get out of the ring. Then they all discharge into the centre at once and burn out, their husks dropping and smoking.
  • Weak window. Spent, it hunches, and its coat-tails slowly open to hatch the next swarm.
  • The full bout. In phase two it stands en garde, the baton a crackling rapier, a line at chest height (dash back), and lunges the length of the room in one stride. It drives the rod into the floor and the current runs out along it both ways (jump). Weak window: grounded and drained, hunched over its rod, before it pulls it free.
6 · The Metronome, the Crawlers and the Overload · lightning on a beat

The Ladder

Meat grafted into machinery. A bloated, stitched body of pale flesh strapped into a brass harness, a stained leather surgeon's apron hanging off it, a welding mask pushed up on its crown. Its face is a cage of ribs that swings open like a gate on a single wet lens, an iris of brass blades focusing in and out. Two antennae of copper rails rise from it and four long railed legs carry it; up the antennae and down the legs the sparks climb, widening as they go, a ladder of lightning.

idle
  • The ladders. On every pair of rails a spark is always climbing: up the antennae, widening between the rails as it rises, and down each leg into the floor, where it fizzles. Its lens's iris closes to a pinhole each time it takes aim.
  • The metronome. The spark reaches the top of the antennae exactly on the beat, pops, and flies at Elfy as a crescent of lightning: tick, tick, tick, TOCK. The beat is the warning, a pulse at its feet before each one, and the height alternates: high (crouch), low (jump), high (crouch). Then the TOCK, a big one, and Elfy is electrified.
  • Ground ladder. Its legs glow and the sparks running down them don't stop at the floor: they crawl along it toward Elfy as zigzag sparks, between crescents up high, so she has to read low and high at once.
  • Overload. It squats and swells, every ladder racing at once, the rib gate rattling; then it slams down and a ring of arcs races out along the floor both ways. Jump it.
  • Weak window. Spent, it vents steam from its seams and its rib gate hangs wide open on the lens.
  • The full bout. In phase two it hops toward Elfy on its four rails, rears with its antennae crackling, the landing outlined under her (dash under), and pounces, slamming its whole body down; a ring of arcs runs both ways (jump). Weak window: sprawled, the rib gate hanging open on the lens. It leaps home over her, its arc dotted (get low).
7 · Touch, Uncorked and the Thunderclap · a storm in a glass, with wings

Lady Nimbus

A lady of the storm, floating. Her head is a glass globe with a living storm trapped inside, and like a plasma globe its lightning reaches for the glass wherever something comes near: it always points at Elfy. She has no face of her own; a face shows only for the frame the storm flashes, in silhouette, a different one each time. A Victorian bodice with a high lace collar and a cameo, long black gloves, a veil hanging from the globe, and below the waist her gown is the storm cloud itself, raining. And wings of storm-feathers, bruise-dark with violet edges, every quill lit white when it lightnings.

idle
  • Touch. Warning: the lightning in her globe bunches against the glass on Elfy's side, brighter and thicker, and a ring marks the floor. Then it punches through the glass as a bolt; the glass cracks a little.
  • Uncorked. She lifts the crystal stopper off the top of her head and the storm pours out and spreads across the ceiling, glowing from inside. Warning: lanes light up, glowing patches under the cloud and on the floor. Then lightning strikes down them in a ripple; where a lane crosses the lava river, molten rock is thrown up. Stand in the gap.
  • Thunderclap. The resurrection wings: an arm that folds and spreads, every feather posed on its own. Warning: she rises, her wings lift high and fold far back, and the storm in her globe goes dark and silent. Then one great downbeat, with motion trails: a white flash, a shock ring, a gust that throws Elfy back across the floor and drives every ember sideways. Feathers tear loose and drift down charged, lying on the floor sizzling.
  • Weak window. Her wings are ragged, bald in patches, and grow back feather by feather.
  • Later in the fight. A wingbeat while the storm is still spread across the ceiling drags the lightning lanes sideways, so the safe gap moves.
  • The full bout. In phase two she climbs high, wings folding tight, her dive's line and landing marked (dash past), and dives head first, glass and all. The glass cracks on the floor and the storm runs out along it both ways (jump). Weak window: she lies there, the storm leaking out of the cracked glass, before she lifts herself home.

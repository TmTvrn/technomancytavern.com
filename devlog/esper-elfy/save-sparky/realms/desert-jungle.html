Realm 2: The Desert Jungle

A new setting for water: a desert at dusk with a jungle grown through it, mesas and a low sun, a river of lava glowing across the middle distance, palms and hanging vines, sand that darkens where water touches it. Drawn at the game's pixel scale, with Elfy for size. Realm 1: the winter studies.

1 · The Wave and the Piranhas · living water, coral and pearl

Tide Contessa

An elegant woman of moving water: an hourglass figure that ripples with light and bubbles, a coral corset trimmed with pearls, a scallop-shell tiara, long hair of streaming water that breaks into drops, and a skirt that pours down into the pool she rises from. She is never still. She sends a great wave, then piranhas out of her own body, then laughs.

idle
  • Always in motion. She sways in an S-curve, light ripples down her body and catches in sparkles, her hair streams back in long strands that drip from the tips, her skirt swirls into the pool and the pool rings.
  • The wave. Warning: she raises both arms, water and sand are drawn up into her and the pool swells, the wave's outline stands in front of her at its full height, and a wet line races across the sand along its path. Then she shoves both hands forward and a curling wave rolls the length of the room, a dark barrel under a lip of foam, spray off the top, lace trailing behind, the sand wet where it passed. Jump its crest.
  • The piranhas. Warning: she arches back with her arms spread, and you can see the fish circling inside her. Then they burst out of her one after another, silver-teal backs and red bellies, jaws snapping, and leap at where Elfy stood. They hit the sand, flop and flip, and dissolve into wet patches.
  • Ohohoho. Back of her hand to her mouth, eyes shut in happy arcs, leaning back. Every "ho" bounces her whole body, her bust a beat behind, and little HO letters bubble up and drift away.
  • The full bout. Then her riptide lash: she draws her arm back coiling water while a line blinks at head height, and a whip cracks the length of the room (crouch). In phase two she pours herself into her pool; a ring hunts Elfy along the sand and she erupts on a geyser where Elfy stood (dash out), then crashes down so crests roll out both ways (jump). Weak window: she melts into a puddle, two eyes blinking up, before welling up again in her pool.
2 · Geysers and the Drain · orb, geyser and whirlpool

Drowned Bather

Goofy from across the room, horrible up close. A gangly thing with a pot belly in a striped bathing costume, an inflatable life ring round its waist, skinny bowed legs and big flapping swim fins. On a long thin neck, a brass-collared diving helmet that is a sloshing orb of water, and floating inside it, its face: a pale drowned fish-thing with bulging, red-veined eyes that roll, a lipless mouth of needle teeth, a glowing lure. It stomps geysers up under you, then opens a whirlpool in the sand and drags you in.

idle
  • Geysers. It lifts a fin high and stomps, sand spraying, three times. Each stomp marks wherever Elfy stands: the sand darkens and burbles with bubbles and little plumes, and a dashed outline rises to show the column. Then a geyser blasts up, a churning foam head on a column of water, drops raining back down, steam, the sand left wet. Keep moving.
  • The drain. It hunches, arms out for a hug, the face in its helmet gaping. Spirals start turning in the sand in front of it. Then the floor opens: foam arms spiral into a dark eye, and sand, drops and you are all dragged in toward its fins. Elfy runs and still slides closer. It closes with a gulp and a burst of spray.
  • Goofy. The fins flap, the belly wobbles in its stripes, the life ring squeaks white and red, and after it drains you it does a little jig, fins slapping the sand. The water in its helmet sloshes with every stomp; its eyes never stop rolling.
  • The full bout. Then the belly flop: it squats, its landing outlined on the sand, leaps, turns over in the air and slams down on its belly (dash under). The flop cracks its helmet (phase two, a jet spurting from the crack). It bounces home on its life ring in three hops, each landing outlined (run under the high one), and lands into three geysers at once (stand in the gap). Weak window: its helmet runs dry and the fish-face gasps in the empty glass.
3 · Eye Bubbles and the Downpour · bubbles, fountain, waterfall, rain

Bishop Bubblegulp

A fat axolotl-toad wizard sitting cross-legged in a stone fountain basin that floats on glowing runes, spilling two little waterfalls onto the sand. Frilly pink gills that glow at the tips, bulging gold eyes, a goofy grin, and a third eye in its brow that splits open when it casts. A peacock robe with gold runes and a high collar, a tall crooked hat whose tip is a fountain spout, and a bubble-wand for a staff.

idle
  • Always busy. It bobs in its basin over a ring of runes, its gills flutter, its eyes blink out of step, its hat sprays water in arcs, its tongue lolls out now and then, and the basin keeps overflowing in two little waterfalls that splash and wet the sand.
  • Eye bubbles. Warning: it dips its wand in the basin, the loop fills with a shimmering rainbow film, the third eye opens and its cheeks puff out. Then it blows five big wobbling rainbow bubbles, each with an eyeball inside it staring at Elfy, drifting toward her. They pop in a spray, the eyes fall out and burst on the sand.
  • The downpour. Warning: it raises its staff, the hat's fountain surges up in a spiral and a storm cloud spreads over the room; then a drop shadow pulses on the sand in every lane but one. Then glowing rain hammers down those lanes, each drop landing in a little crown, the sand turning to puddles. Stand in the gap.
  • Croak. Its throat sac swells into a bubble as big as its head and pops, CROAK, and it claps its webbed hands.
  • The full bout. Then the basin ram: the basin tilts back, runes flaring, its path lit along the floor, and it charges the length of the room into the wall (double jump over its hat). From the wall in phase two its tongue lashes across the room at head height (crouch), then it bounces bubbles along the floor, each landing outlined (double jump). Weak window: it floats home, the basin tips into the sand and it sees stars.
4 · The Bridge, the Relay and the Boil · two tiny water assassins at a lava ford

Siphon & Sever

A pair that only works together, at a ford where the floor is cut by a pit of lava. Siphon is the caster: a floating thing in ultraviolet rags with a hood swept back to a point, a bone beak-mask with one cold eye and a pinprick for the other, a glass flask of glowing brine for a heart, and no legs, only a dripping rope of water. Sever is the blade: a reverse-kneed runt in midnight armor, a crest of chrome fins, hot pink eyes, a long yellow scarf, and a whip of brine on its wrist. Neither ever stands still.

idle
  • The lava is the third enemy. A pit in the floor between Elfy and the pair, always spitting embers, lighting its walls, the sand scorched black at its lip. They use water to change it: harden it, cross it, boil it.
  • The bridge and the lash. Warning: the bridge's outline blinks over the pit while Siphon's flask glows. Siphon pours brine from its beak in a sweep and the lava hisses into a glowing crust that cools to obsidian. Sever coils, eyes flaring, and a line skims the sand at shin height. Then it sprints across, leaving afterimages, skids, and snaps the whip low. Jump. It backflips home, and the bridge cracks and sinks.
  • The relay. Siphon bursts into water and re-forms high up, claws raised, drawing brine out of the air into a sphere. Sever coils under it and three lines reach for Elfy's head. Siphon drops it, Sever leaps and front-flips and whips it, and it bursts into three crescent blades that scream over the lava, steam hissing under them. Crouch.
  • The boil. Siphon floats over the pit and empties its heart into the lava. The lava heaves, the outline of a pillar rises over it and rings mark the sand where it will land; Sever retreats spinning its whip. Then the pit blows: a pillar of steam lit orange, lava thrown in arcs onto both shores, scorch that glows and cools. Step back.
  • The pair. Sever flips up and perches on Siphon's hood, crouched, scarf streaming, eyes flaring in time; then drops off and they start again.
  • The full bout. In phase two Sever curls into a spin dash, its path lit along the floor: it rolls, hops the pit, hits the wall and rebounds high over Elfy (jump the roll). Siphon rises over her, its landing outlined, and dives (dash out), a crest running out from the slam (jump). Weak window: both spent, seeing stars.
5 · The Lightning Call · a prism jellyfish at the oasis

The Thunderbell

A jellyfish grown into a monster, floating over the jungle floor of a desert oasis while a volcano bleeds lava on the horizon. Its bell is see-through and banded in every color, sliding through them as it pulses; a gold circlet round its rim hangs with charms. Its face never stops making faces. Two of its tentacles have grown into long arms with clawed hands; the rest trail under it, glinting with charge. It raises a claw, marks Elfy with a sigil, darkens the sky, and calls the lightning down on her.

idle
  • The scene. Still the desert, at dusk: a volcano on the horizon with lava pulsing down its flanks and a plume lit red from inside; a little oasis in the dunes, a pool ringed with reeds and leaning palms; and the floor overgrown with jungle, broad split leaves, ferns, tall grass, pink and gold flowers, fireflies.
  • The face. Big eyes that track Elfy and blink, thick brows that carry its mood, a mouth that smirks, snarls a grin of needle teeth, roars wide with a long blue tongue, then laughs with its eyes squeezed shut and its cheeks flushed. Its eyes flash white with the strike.
  • The arms. Its left and right tentacles are arms: long, ropy, prismatic, ending in hands with four clawed fingers that splay, clutch, crook into a claw while it calls the storm, and point when it brings the bolt down.
  • Warning. The claw goes up crackling, a thread of static running from it into the sky; a sigil of turning dashes draws itself on the sand under Elfy and charges brighter, static rising off it; then the whole sky goes dark and the leader feels its way down in steps while a streamer climbs out of the sigil to meet it.
  • The strike. On the downswing a thick bolt slams into Elfy, then two more strokes down the same channel. She jolts: flashes blue-white, her outline lit through on the brightest flickers, sparks crawling over her, thrown back a step with each stroke, shivering. Cracks race out across the sand, glow with heat and cool to scorch; she kneels, smoking, and gets back up.
  • Then it laughs. HA HA bubbling up out of its mouth, its bell bouncing, its hands clutching itself.
  • The full bout. In phase two it drifts in close, then drops low with its tentacles live while the floor lights up the length of the room, and sweeps into the far wall (double jump over it). From the wall it calls a chain: a sigil, then a bolt, three of them walking after Elfy (run ahead). Weak window: overcharged, it slumps on the sand, fizzing, eyes rolled shut.
6 · The Coconut Bomb · a sad robot archer at the oasis

The Weeping Palm

A palm tree that is a robot, and is very sad about it. A riveted bronze trunk in rings like a palm's, slumped forward; a little screen for a face with amber eyes that droop at the corners and leak oil; a crown of patina-green metal fronds that hang down like a willow's; six roots it walks on like a spider. On its back, a big woven quiver of coconut bombs with fuses. One of its boughs is a bow. It is good at this, and it isn't happy about that either.

idle
  • Sad and droopy. Its fronds hang heavier when it sighs, a puff of steam from the vent in its crown, a little ellipsis floating up. Its amber eyes sag at the corners, its brows lift in the middle, its frown wobbles, and every so often a drop of oil runs down its screen. After it hits Elfy it feels worse.
  • Root legs. Six roots in two tripods, brass knuckles at the joints, tips curled to dig in, scuttling it in and out like a spider and scuffing the sand.
  • The shot (after the pixelfx arrow shot). It reaches back into the quiver for a coconut, flicks a spark to light the fuse, raises the bow, nocks, draws to its face, and holds its breath, trembling, straightened for once. Warning: while it holds, the lob's arc is dotted out and a ring marks the sand at Elfy's feet. Then the string snaps forward and the coconut lobs, spinning, fuse spitting sparks.
  • The fuse. It lands at her feet and fizzes, the fuse burning down, a dashed dome of the blast blinking faster and faster. She turns to run, too late.
  • The hit (after the pixelfx meteor call and rocket launcher). The longest freeze, a white flash, a fireball that swells and rises, a shock ring along the sand, the hardest shake, shell and sand and embers thrown, a column of smoke. Elfy is thrown up and back, lands on one knee charred black with embers glowing in the soot, smoking, and only slowly comes clean again before she walks back.
  • The full bout. It has had enough: its screen goes red and it flings three bombs at once, each landing ringed, each blast dome blinking (dash out from under all three). Then it rears back, its crown's reach outlined on the sand, and throws its whole body down so the crown slams the floor (back off). Weak window: its crown is stuck in the sand; hit its face before it wrenches free.
7 · Chords · bubbles, ripples and a waterfall from a huge crab

Abbot Barnacle, the Brine Organ

A colossal hermit crab filling a third of the room, standing in a tide pool of its own making. Its shell is a cathedral pipe organ grown out of coral and barnacles. Two eyes on long stalks wear tiny round spectacles; it has a great crusher claw and a spindly one it conducts with, and a moth-eaten choir robe hemmed in gold draped over its legs. It plays itself: every attack is a chord.

idle
  • Every move is animated. Anticipation: before each chord it rises and draws breath, its shell stretching tall, its spindly claw lifting like a baton. Squash and stretch: on the chord it slams down, the shell squashing wide, the pipes stretching as they blow. Follow-through: it overshoots and settles on a spring, and its eyestalks whip and wobble long after the body stops. Slow in and out on every pose, a snap on every strike, and arcs for the claw.
  • The bubble sheet. Warning: a band of sand under Elfy fizzes and its edges are outlined up to where the bubbles will go off. Then a curtain of bubbles rises, every size, wobbling and swelling as they climb, lit rims and see-through bodies. The first to reach the top pop, each one a white flash, a ring and a spray, and sets off its neighbours a beat later: the whole sheet goes off like a string of firecrackers. Three chords, three sheets, each aimed where Elfy went.
  • The ripple drum. It lifts its legs under the robe (anticipation), slams them down, and a glossy crest of water runs out along the sand, three in a row, on the beat. Jump each one.
  • The fire hose. Warning: it rears the crusher claw back low, bubbles gurgling at its tip, and a dotted aim line runs from the claw to Elfy. Then it thrusts the claw straight out and fires a pressurised jet like a fireman's hose: a glassy rod with surges running down it, sagging under its own weight, roughening and breaking into chunks toward the far end. It blasts Elfy back across the sand in a burst of spray, and the kick shoves the crab back on its legs, the claw shuddering with the pressure.
  • A Muppet mouth. The top of its carapace is hinged at the back and lifts off the mouth line like a Muppet's head: shut for a breath before each chord, then flung wide to sing it; a chomp on every drum; gritted while it hoses; and after it blasts Elfy it flaps with laughter, mouth dark, tongue and little teeth showing, its eyestalks riding up and down on the lid.
  • Weak window. Played out, its eyestalks droop all the way down to the sand and it wipes its spectacles with a little cloth.
  • The full bout. In phase two it scuttles in and rears its crusher claw high, its landing outlined (dash back), and smashes it into the sand. Stuck, it panics into a full organ: every pipe at once, bubble bombs lobbed onto ringed spots (stand in the gap). Weak window: spent, pipes venting steam, claw buried; jump the claw and hit its face before it wrenches free.
8 · Mines, the Drop and the Wash · a diving bell riding its own water jet

Captain Sump

A portly brass diving bell in a navy admiral's coat, gold epaulettes and a bicorne, floating on a pressurised jet of water from a nozzle centered under its body. Its face is a porthole with one huge magnified eye and a brass shutter for an eyelid, under kelp brows and over a great drooping kelp mustache; two octopus tentacles curl out of its rivet holes.

idle
  • Always on the jet. It bobs on the pressure surges, the bell squashing and stretching with them; a foaming skirt boils where the jet lands; its epaulette fringe, mustache, tentacles and hat all swing a beat behind every move.
  • Bilge mines. It puffs up tall (anticipation), then bloops three water orbs out of its side vents, squashing with each one. They float out in arcs toward Elfy, wobbling, the floor showing upside down through them, a burst ring around each blinking faster before they go off in a crown of spray.
  • Jet drop. It climbs over Elfy in an arc, a target marks the sand, the jet sputters and coughs. Then it cuts: it hangs a beat looking down, falls stretched, slams down with the hardest shake, and two crests of water skim out both ways. Jump them.
  • Weak window. Jet gone, it bounces across the sand in squashing hops, hat flopping, eye spinning behind the glass, until it re-pressurises with a cough and a whoosh and shoots back up.
  • Point-blank. Re-pressurised, it flies in close to Elfy, winds back the other way (anticipation), then swings round until its nozzle faces her, overshooting and settling, while an aim line blinks. Then it fires: the jet slams into her and drives her back across the sand, and the recoil hurls the captain away across the room. It swings back upright with a wobble, its hat bouncing on its own spring.
  • The full bout. In phase two it swings its nozzle round backward, sputters while a corridor at head height lights across the room (crouch), and torpedoes the length of the room on its own jet into the wall. Weak window: crumpled on the sand, porthole cracked, eye spinning, before it re-pressurises and flies home.
9 · Plugholes, Teapots and a Leaky Ceiling · a tiny hag who travels by whirlpool

Little Mother Drain

A tiny hag, knee-high to Elfy, made of wet hair and a ladle: a soaked lace bonnet with its ties hanging, a knit cardigan with too many pockets, all of it dripping; two pinprick lights for eyes and a gummy grin with one tooth; a bathtub plug on a chain dragging behind her. She never walks. She goes down one whirlpool and comes up another. Here, under a rock overhang that drips.

idle
  • Plughole hop. Warning: foam starts turning in the sand beside Elfy. She squashes down (anticipation), hops up stretched, and spins down into her whirlpool; then shoots up out of the new one stretched tall, squashes, and winds the ladle back before swinging it round in a big arc with a smear. Its whirlpool pulls at Elfy while she's beside it, so she has to run against it.
  • Teapot fountains. She slaps the sand with the ladle three times, squashing on each slap, and a porcelain teapot pops up at each: their landing rings show on the sand first. Then they bloom: a jet out of each spout throwing glassy arcs that break into beads and rain down on the rings in little plinks.
  • Leaky ceiling. She shakes her ladle at the rock overhead, trembling with it. Drips swell on the ceiling, sagging and catching the light, their shadows growing on the sand; then they pinch off one after another and fall stretched by their speed, landing in crowns, leaving one dry spot.
  • Weak window. She can't use the same whirlpool twice in a row, so she spins down to come up somewhere new, and that somewhere is right under Elfy. She bonks her head on Elfy's foot, squashes flat, and sits there seeing stars before she sinks away and comes up home.
  • Follow-through. Her curtain of wet hair, the bonnet ties and the plug on its chain all swing a beat behind every hop, slap and spin.
  • The full bout. In phase two she squats and starts to turn, ladle out, the floor lit to the wall, and goes off like a spinning top across the sand (jump her), ricochets off the wall and spins back (jump again). Weak window: dizzy and wobbling, seeing stars, before she sinks away home.

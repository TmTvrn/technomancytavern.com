Realm 4: The Rust Orchard

Blades in autumn. A wood at dusk grown through the wreck of something enormous that fell out of the sky: crimson and gold trees leaning over rusted hull plates still blinking with cold teal lights, god-rays slanting through the canopy, fog at three depths, fireflies low. A catwalk bolted to the hull, a great bough and a gantry hung on chains make the room three storeys tall, and the enemies here use all of it. The leaves never stop falling: they swing down and settle in drifts, whirl in every gust, crunch up under every landing, and every blade that passes through them cuts them in two. Drawn at the game's pixel scale, with Elfy for size. Realm 1 · Realm 2 · Realm 3.

1 · Reap, Leaf Storm, Flurry · a scythe-armed mantis-scarecrow on the boughs

The Harvester

A mantis-scarecrow built out of rust and wire: a spine of iron, long reverse-jointed legs that throw it from bough to gantry to catwalk, a harvest-festival coat in tatters flapping round it, and two arms that end in scythes, folded in front of it like a mantis praying. It has no head to speak of, only a little projector on a stalk throwing a hologram of a carved gourd's face in front of itself, scanlined, flickering, glitching from one grin to the next.

idle
  • The reap. Warning: on its bough it rears both scythes back high behind it, leaning away and coiling, its face snapping to a glare, while the crescent of the reap is dotted out in the air below. Then it swings: a huge, slow, dark crescent with a pale ghostly edge reaps round from the bough, hangs, and tears away from its tail in curling wisps. Every leaf it passes through is cut in two. Get out of its reach.
  • Leaf storm. It crouches, springs and lands on the hanging gantry, setting it swinging. Leaves spiral up round it in bands of wind and lift it off its feet; four lines reach from it to the floor; then it flings razor leaves down them, spinning and glinting, shredding the leaves they pass. Get off the floor: Elfy jumps up onto the catwalk.
  • Flurry and the cut. It drops onto her catwalk, the walkway sagging, and its scythes become a blur: a storm of little crescents at every angle, sparks off each. Its reach is marked along the walkway. Then it rears back once more and thrusts: a long glinting streak down the catwalk with a star at its tip, and Elfy is knocked off.
  • Weak window. Its face glitches out into static, the projector spitting sparks, its scythes drooping, before it springs home to the bough.
  • The full bout. In phase two it curls up, scythes out, the floor under the catwalk lit (get up on the catwalk), drops off the bough and comes down the room as a reaping wheel of blades. It bites into the far wall. Weak window: stuck there, its face gone to static; drop down and hit it before it tears free and springs home over Elfy (get low).
2 · Thorns, the Whip and the Snare · a spider-drone that lives upside-down

Thornwire

A spider that was a maintenance drone once: a cage of rusted ribs round a dim red core, wrapped in coils of barbed wire, and briars have grown through all of it, thorns and all. Eight legs of wire with hooked tips. Where a head should be, a bunch of camera lenses like dark berries, each catching the light, all turning together to look at you. It lives upside-down under the boughs and catwalks and comes down on its own cable.

idle
  • It's almost never where you can reach it. Under the bough, under the catwalk, swinging between them on its cable like a pendulum, legs drawn in, then snatching the underside of the next platform and settling with a bounce.
  • Thorns. Warning: it rears against the bough, its lenses ringed teal and glaring red, and the seam's path glows along the floor. It drops a cable to the floor and a seam races out from it, glowing, and hooked metal thorns punch up behind it one after another, each bigger than the last, throwing up leaves and dirt. They're too big to jump by the end: get up onto the catwalk.
  • The whip. From under the catwalk it creeps to the edge, and a ring of red settles on Elfy. A loop rolls down its thorned cable as it curls up over the edge, and the tip cracks where she stood with a white pop, shredding the leaves. Drop off.
  • The snare. The floor cracks round her in a glowing ring; cables burst up and spiral round her legs and cinch with a snap. It drops off the catwalk and scuttles in, rearing to pounce. She tears free just in time and vaults it.
  • Weak window. It pounces on empty floor and lands on its back, legs flailing, sparks spitting, lenses blind, until it rights itself and reels itself home on its cable.
  • The full bout. In phase two it creeps along the bough, its swing's arc dotted and its landing outlined (dash), and swings down on its cable to slam where Elfy stood. Its reach is ringed, then it whirls a barbed lasso at shin height (jump). Weak window: tangled in its own wire, legs flailing, lenses blind, before it reels itself home.
3 · Quake, Boulder, Spikes, the Fall · a digging machine that breaks the room

The Quakewright

A digging machine that learned to walk and to hate. A hulking golem of rusted plate on two piston legs, hazard stripes worn to rust down its edges, a drill for one fist and a sledgehammer for the other. Its head is an excavator's cab: two amber headlamps for eyes behind a cracked windscreen, and a toothed bucket for a jaw. In its chest a plasma core beats in a steel cage, throwing a ring of light on every beat. It doesn't climb the platforms; it breaks them.

idle
  • The quake. Warning: it raises both fists high and rears up on its legs, its bucket jaw dropping, while the floor flashes. Then it slams down: three jolts, each harder, the room shaking, a rift tearing out along the floor glowing molten deep inside, the slabs heaving up and dropping askew, pebbles hopping, every leaf thrown into the air. The floor is death; ride it out on the catwalk.
  • The boulder. It drives its drill into the floor in a spray of grit, tears out a boulder and lifts it overhead in both fists while a ring settles on Elfy's catwalk. It throws: the boulder arcs over, shedding dust, and smashes into the catwalk, which buckles and swings down from its last bolts. Get off first.
  • Earth spikes. It punches the drill into the floor and leaves it spinning, and a glowing crack opens under Elfy, then the next place she'll be, then the next: faceted stone spikes ram up with shards at their sides, one after another, chasing her across the room.
  • The fall. It crouches deep and leaps onto the hanging gantry. The chains strain, sparks fly, and they snap: the golem and the gantry come down together in the biggest crash in the fight, leaves and wreckage everywhere.
  • Weak window. Overheated, it kneels in the wreck, steam venting, its cage doors swung open on a white-hot core spitting arcs, until it rises and the cage closes.
  • The full bout. In phase two it crouches deep, core flaring, its landing outlined under Elfy (dash), and leaps the whole room, driving itself into the floor. Its core flares and a ring of plasma runs out along the floor (jump). Weak window: half-buried, its cage open on the core. It tears itself out and leaps home over her (get low).
4 · Ricochet, the X and the Pin · a pair of blade drones, and a wall to cling to

The Cutter Kites

Two blade drones shaped like the seeds of a maple: a faceted pod with a single lens for an eye and one long curved wing that is a blade. They drift on the air the way the real seeds do, spinning, and then they don't drift. They're cold against the autumn, from the far side of the colour wheel: midnight-blue steel with cyan edges, one with a cyan eye, one violet. They hunt as a pair. This corner of the wreck has a sheer bulkhead for a wall, a crane hook swinging a pallet across the room, and a plate bolted high on the hull; Elfy uses the wall.

idle
  • Ricochet. Warning: one rises, winds back and glints, and its whole path is dotted out across the room with every bounce ringed. Then it whirls down it, a blurred disc of blade, bouncing off the floor and the hook in flashes and rings, every leaf in its path cut in two. Elfy runs to the wall, jumps onto it, clings and slides down it after it passes.
  • The X. Warning: they part to two corners and wind back along two lines that cross on Elfy, her spot marked. Then they dart: two strokes closing on the crossing, flashing white as they meet, a burst of cyan and violet sparks. Elfy is up the wall, clinging between the two lines, and slides down after.
  • The pin. One plants its blade in the plate bolted high on the hull; the other stabs into the wall. A beam flickers between them and locks: the upper storey is shut. Then the wall one drags its end down the wall, gouging sparks out of the rust, and the beam scissors down across the room; get under its high end. Then the plate one tears loose and dives at her.
  • Weak window. The wall one has driven itself into the rust too deep: jammed, twisting, eye flickering, while its twin circles it, until it tears free.
  • The full bout. In phase two they lock together into one four-bladed saw, its whole ricochet dotted out and every bounce ringed (get up on the hook), and it ricochets floor, wall, floor, shredding everything it passes, then bites into the floor and jams. Weak window: jammed, both eyes flickering, before they tear apart and rise.
5 · Spores, the Beam and the Warp · a floating priest of the wreck

The Spore Abbot

A censer that became a priest that became a toadstool. A great domed cap of indigo steel studded with cyan lamps, violet light glowing up through the gills beneath it; under the brim, in shadow, a brass monocle lens and a grille for a mouth; a moth-eaten robe of midnight velvet hanging below with no feet in it, hovering on a faint cold glow; thin jointed arms, one swinging a censer on a chain. It plants its children, it drips, it lifts you up and lets you go, and it steps from pad to pad in a column of light. Here the wall is on the right, the platforms carry teleporter pads, and the fog lies thicker.

idle
  • Cold against the autumn. Indigo, cyan and violet, the far side of the colour wheel from the leaves, so it never sinks into the scenery.
  • Spore toadstools. Warning: it winds its censer back and flings seeds, and rings mark where they'll land. Steel toadstools push up out of the floor, swell and tremble, their gills glowing brighter, then squash and puff lumpy clouds of glowing violet spores that billow up, drift and settle. Get above them: Elfy runs to the wall, jumps onto it and clings there, sliding slowly.
  • The warp. A column of light: it flickers, flashes white and streams up off its pad, and slams down on the high plate right beside her, painting itself back in from the top. She kicks off the wall and flips away.
  • Drip and the beam. It drifts over her dripping tar, a puddle of black veined with violet that bubbles. Warning: a cone of light is outlined round her. Then the tractor beam: leaves and grit tear loose and spiral up into its lens, rings of light climb, and Elfy is lifted and carried over the tar and let go into it, poisoned.
  • Weak window. It warps back to its pad and sags there, lamps out, its lens cracked, rings of light rising off the pad into it as it recharges.
  • The full bout. In phase two it drifts down close and lets its censer out on a long chain, the whole circle of its reach dotted (get up on the plate), and whirls it round itself in a great circle, then slams it into the floor; spores roll out both ways. Weak window: its chain snagged, it sags, lamps out, lens cracked.
6 · Sail Sweep, Leaf Tornado, Lance, Claw · the boss of the orchard

The Gale Warden

A knight the size of a mill. Plate of blue-black steel, a violet tabard, a cape torn into ribbons that never stops moving; for a head, a little mill tower with a conical violet cap, and on its face four sails of pale canvas on a lattice round a hub with one cyan eye. A jousting lance striped cyan in one hand, a gauntlet with three talons on the other. Everything about it is wind, and it fights like the last act of a shonen fight: every strike is a held breath, focus lines closing in on the wind-up, and then a gale and a two-tone impact frame.

idle
  • Sail sweep. Anticipation the wrong way round: its sails slow, stop, and wind backwards while it leans away, and they telescope out to three times their length; the reach is ringed. Then it bows and the sails whirl through the floor in a white blur, sparking where they graze it, cutting every leaf in reach. They overshoot, spring back and retract.
  • Leaf tornado. It plants its feet and its sails blur; a path is marked to her catwalk. A funnel of wind writhes loose and wanders across the room, tearing up the leaves into it. It catches Elfy and carries her up a storey, spinning, and throws her onto the gantry, so the next attack can reach her.
  • Lance. It coils, sails locked in an X, lance drawn back, a glint at its tip, focus lines closing in; the line of the thrust is marked across her storey. She drops. It leaps, after-images trailing, and a spear of wind runs the whole width of the room, with an impact frame and the gantry swinging on its chains. It lands hard.
  • The claw. It rears its gauntlet high and back, three arcs marked over her; then the swipe over the top and down, three violet crescents that hang and tear away, and this one catches her.
  • Weak window. All that wind has a cost: leaves stream into its sails and choke them. They judder and grind to a stop, the hub sparking and smoking, and it drops to one knee, lance planted, head bowed, eye guttering. Then it shakes them out in a burst of leaves and rises.
  • The full bout. In phase two, storm form: it lifts off on its sails, a corridor at head height lit across the room (crouch), and flies the room, sails a blur. It wheels at the wall and comes down lance first; a ring of wind bursts out along the floor (jump). Weak window: spent, kneeling on its planted lance, sails stopped. It leaps home over Elfy (get low).

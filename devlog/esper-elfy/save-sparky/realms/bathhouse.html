Realm 8: The Last Bathhouse

A public bathhouse long closed, at midnight, still being kept by its ghosts. Dark cedar pillars and beams, indigo-and-cream tiles, a great faded mural of a mountain over waves on the back wall, red paper lanterns, steam always rolling; and the soaking pool sunk in the middle of the floor is full of glowing lime acid, an arched footbridge over it, wooden lofts either side. Mint is spectral, lime is acid, and both are kept well clear of Elfy's own saturated green: mint leans cyan and pale, lime leans yellow. Realm 7: the ossuary chapel.

1 · Bucket Splash, Behind You, Soul Drain, the Sheet · spectral

The Attendant

The ghost who still keeps the bathhouse: a tall woman in a white yukata patterned in indigo, a wide sash, long black hair down over her face with one mint eye showing through, a towel over her shoulder and a cedar bucket bound in copper. She has no feet; below the hem she trails off into mist. She is mint-washed and see-through, and you can't hurt mist.

idle
  • Bucket splash. She drifts to the pool's edge and dips her bucket in the acid; three rings mark the floor. She swings and throws, three gobs of glowing acid in arcs, and they land on the rings and lie there fizzing and steaming. Get up on the loft.
  • Behind you (after the pixelfx blink-out and blink-in). She sinks through the floor. A mint ripple spreads on the loft right behind Elfy, and the towel's line is drawn across it. She rises out of the boards and snaps her towel across the whole loft. Drop off.
  • Soul drain (after the pixelfx soul-drain). She reaches, and a tether of mint runs from her hand to Elfy: Elfy is pulled toward her, and her colour drains out of her and runs along the tether, until she dashes far enough to snap it: the bridge.
  • The sheet (phase two). She comes up at her station and snaps her towel out into a sheet of spectral cloth that sweeps the footbridge: first at head height (crouch), then at the ankles (jump), each line flickering first.
  • Weak window. She overdoes it and turns solid: opaque, colour in her yukata, her eye gone dark, slumped and dazed. Solid things can be hit. Elfy cuts her three times before she fades back to mist.
2 · Spit, Belly Flop, Slimelets, the Whirlpool · acid

The Soak

The bath water gone bad. A great wobbling blob of lime acid that lives in the pool and is as big as the pool, with two bubble eyes and a wide dripping mouth; everything that ever fell in is still floating inside it, a bath stool, a bucket, a sandal, some bones, and at its heart a rubber duck. The duck is what it really is.

idle
  • The spit. It swells, its mouth opening wide, and three rings mark the floor; then it spits three gobs of acid in arcs that splash on the rings and fizz. Get up on the loft.
  • The belly flop. It rears up out of the pool, stretching tall and leaning, its landing outlined on the floor; then it flops onto the floor in a crash of acid, wobbling flat, rings and spray both ways, steam coming off it, and slides back into the pool.
  • Slimelets (after the pixelfx slime). It shudders and splits into three little slimes that hop out after Elfy, each landing ringed first: one onto her loft (drop off it), one onto the floor where she lands (jump for the bridge), one off to the far side. They squash and wobble, then hop home and merge.
  • The whirlpool (phase two) (after the pixelfx black-hole). It sinks, and the pool turns into a spinning vortex: everything on the floor and the bridge is dragged toward its centre. Elfy has to run against the pull off the bridge to the far floor. A column is outlined, and it erupts in a geyser of acid and comes down.
  • Weak window. It collapses into a thin crust over the pool, and its heart pops out: the rubber duck, landing dazed on the floor. Elfy hits the duck three times; it dives home and the Soak swells back up.
3 · Shiko, the Salt, the Tachiai, the Tsuppari, the Bow · acid, sumo

Ozeki Kappa

A river imp who is also a champion wrestler: river-stone blue skin, a tortoise shell on its back, a yellow beak, a red mawashi with a fringe of cords, and on top of its head, in a ring of dark hair, the dish every kappa has, full to the brim with lime acid from the pool. It is enormously strong. It is also, like every kappa, terribly polite.

idle
  • Shiko. It slaps its belly, then lifts one leg high, leaning, the floor flickering; it stomps, and a crest runs the length of the floorboards (splashing through the pool on the way). Then the other leg. Jump each one.
  • The salt. It reaches into its mawashi and flings a fistful of purifying salt in a glittering arc onto four rings. Get onto the bridge.
  • The tachiai. It settles into its stance, fists down, the charge's arc and landing marked. Then it charges and leaps the whole pool, right through where the bridge is; double jump over it. It lands on the far side and skids into the wall.
  • The tsuppari (phase two). Angry, steam puffing from its ears, it fires open-palm thrusts along the bridge: white handprints of force, low, high, low, high, each line flickering first. Jump, crouch, jump, crouch.
  • The bow, and the weak window. Elfy bows to it. It cannot help itself: it bows back, deeply, and the acid pours out of its dish. A kappa with an empty dish has no strength: it slumps to its knees, eyes spinning. Elfy hits it three times; it trudges to the pool, scoops its dish full, and leaps home.
4 · The Lick, the Roost, Lights Out, the Dive · spectral fire

Chōchin

One of the bathhouse's own paper lanterns, old enough to have come alive: a ribbed cylinder of yellowed paper between black lacquered rims, split open into a grinning mouth full of paper teeth, one huge eye, a long lolling tongue; and inside it, where the candle should be, a cold mint ghost-fire glowing through the paper. Bats roost in it.

idle
  • The lick. Its mouth splits wide, the paper stretching, and a pink line flickers at head height; then its tongue shoots the length of the room. Crouch.
  • The roost (after the pixelfx bat). Its mouth gapes and the bats come out in a stream, flapping along the floor in a wave, mint eyes glinting. Get up on the loft.
  • Lights out (after the pixelfx wisp). It puffs up and blows the other lanterns out; the room goes black but for its own fire, Elfy and four ghost-fires circling it. They peel off one after another and dive at ringed spots: the loft, the floor, the bridge. Get off the loft. The lights come back.
  • The dive (phase two). It catches fire, mint flames licking up its paper, and rises to the ceiling over Elfy; a mark locks on under it, its eye looking straight down. Then it drops like a stone. Dash. It bounces up, locks on again, and drops again.
  • Weak window. The second dive tears it: it lies crumpled on the floor, the bamboo frame showing through the rips, tongue out, eye spinning. Elfy hits it three times; then it puffs itself out and floats back up.
5 · Lunge, Under the Boards, Stone Spit, the Tail, the Knot · acid

The Drain Wyrm

What lives down the drain: a long serpent of lime acid ringed darker, a pale belly, a flat wedge of a head with big wobbling googly eyes whose pupils rattle round loose, catfish whiskers, and the bath's rubber drain plug hanging from a ring through its nose on its little chain. Its spit sets into stone.

idle
  • The lunge. The pool churns, then a serpent rears out of it, its lunge drawn to the bridge. It strikes the bridge; get off.
  • Under the boards. It dives and swims under the floorboards: they bulge along its path, and its burst is marked where Elfy stands. Get up on the loft; it bursts up through the boards in a spray of splinters.
  • Stone spit (after the pixelfx petrified status). It rears and spits three gobs that go grey as they fall and set into stone pillars where they land: things to stand on, for a while.
  • The tail (phase two). Its tail comes out of the pool and sweeps the far side: first the loft (drop onto the pillar), then the floor (stay up on it).
  • The knot, and the weak window. It rears and lunges at Elfy on her pillar; she jumps over its head, and it lunges so hard it ties itself in a knot, its head flopped on the floor, eyes rattling. Elfy hits it three times; it unties itself and slithers back down the drain, and the pillars crumble.
6 · Abacus, the Doorways, Closing Time, the Beam · spectral · the owner

The Proprietress

The owner, who never left. An enormous mint ghost of a bathhouse matron rising out of the acid behind the footbridge: a dark kimono patterned with waves and a red sash, her hair in a great bun stuck with gold pins and a comb, a round powdered face, rouged cheeks, a beauty mark, and the most expressive eyebrows in the building; they do most of her talking. She keeps the accounts on an abacus and closes up with a hand bell.

idle
  • The beads. A sly look, one brow up; rings mark the bridge and the floors. She flicks a row of beads off her abacus in arcs, and they land and bounce. Get off the bridge.
  • The doorways (after the pixelfx portal). Her brows come down. The left doorway's curtain ripples open on mint light and a mark lands on the loft under it; a giant ghost hand reaches out of the doorway and slams the loft. Then the right doorway: a hand reaches out and sweeps the floor flat, its line flickering first. Drop, then jump for the bridge.
  • Closing time (phase two) (after the pixelfx time-stop). Her brows fly up; she rings her bell and flings acid at the room. Time stops: everything drains to grey under a great clock face, the drops hang in the air, and a dotted line runs from each to the ring where it will land. Only Elfy can move. Get somewhere no line ends. Time starts again and the drops land.
  • The beam (phase three) (after the pixelfx disintegrate). She takes a breath that puffs her cheeks out, eyes squeezed shut; the whole floor flickers. Then she breathes a column of mint light that takes the floorboards apart as it sweeps the room, the boards piecing themselves back together behind it; at the far end it rises up the wall over the loft. Be up, then drop behind it.
  • Weak window. Out of breath, she deflates like a paper balloon to the size of Elfy's head and flops onto the bridge, eyes spinning. Elfy hits her three times; then she puffs herself back up, and smiles.

Save Sparky devlog · realm 1 · not boss patterns

Realm 1: The Blizzard Arena

Enemies whose attacks aren't boss patterns, each with its own fire. Drawn at the game's pixel scale in the blizzard arena, with Elfy for size. Every attack has a warning you can read from across the room: a pose, a sound made visible, a mark on the floor. Realm 2: the desert jungle.

1 · Fire Ring

Brazier Knight

A squat iron knight whose helmet is an open brazier. Its head barely smoulders until it charges: it swings the sledgehammer back, the fire roars, the floor glows where it will land, and the slam sends a Fire Ring racing out along the ground. Jump the wave of flame at the ring's edge.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · lift · wind-up (the warning) · swing · slam · recover
  • Charge. At rest the head only smoulders. As it winds up, the fire roars, the eyes go white, wisps of flame stream into the hammer, and the floor glows where it will land.
  • Slam. The head erupts in a column of fire and the hammer smears down its arc: a white flash, rock chips and sparks, a short shake. After, the bowl is spent and smokes until it rekindles.
  • The wave. Flames are tallest at the ring's left and right edges and burn down fast behind them. Jump the edge and land in the cooling middle.
  • Back edge. In the pixelfx gallery the back of the ring floats about 18 px above the floor; here it lies on the snow. Toggle it to compare.
  • The full bout. Then the bout: bull rush (it drops its head, fire streaming back, the line marked, and charges the length of the room ploughing sparks; Elfy goes up onto the ledge), an uppercut that throws a crescent of fire at the ledge (she flips over him), a leap onto her marked spot that throws up a geyser, and in phase two the head-fire overflows (an aura, an impact frame) into a double slam whose second ring stands twice as tall. Weak window: the hammer sticks in the ground and its fire gutters to smoke.
2 · Fire Tornadoes · orange fire, teal and glass

Kiln Cantor

A living bottle kiln on three bird legs, from a people who treat fire as music. It wears a ceremonial coat of woven heat-proof metal hung with glass chimes, and a collar of tuning forks. It sings fire: a fireball grows on its head and is lobbed straight at you, then eight more follow in a rapid barrage, each smaller and closer, each raising a funnel of fire.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · rear (the warning) · spit · pump (the barrage) · recover
  • Warning. It rears back, arms up like a conductor. The tuning forks turn glass-blue and ring out in waves, sparks are drawn into a fireball growing on its head, and a target pulses on the floor under Elfy, faster as the ball swells.
  • The first shot. At full size the ball is lobbed high, right at Elfy, with a white flash of the silhouette and a freeze. The biggest funnel rises where it lands. Move off the target.
  • The barrage. Eight more, one every tenth of a second, each ball smaller and landing closer to the Cantor, so the funnels step down in size toward it. Each mark flashes just before its ball lands.
  • The funnels. Each forms rather than scales: a ring of flame spins up on the floor, a rope of fire climbs out of it, then the top flares open. They pull snow in round their feet and pinch back to a rope as they die.
  • The full bout. Then: it stomps after her and rams the ledge's post with its shoulder; its forks ring a shockwave she flips through; in phase two its kiln cracks white-hot and it sweeps a stream of fire along the floor and up the low ledge while she hides high behind it. Weak window: overheated, it slumps, steaming.
3 · Star-fire Survey · cold star-fire, faded expedition orange

Ash Cartographer

A six-limbed surveyor from a burned-out world, in a faded orange expedition suit with reflective stripes, map tubes strapped to its back and a folding visor arm. Its seed-pod head splits open to show the star inside. It maps a burn: three stakes, then columns of star-fire.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · aim (the warning) · fire · recover
  • Warning. It crouches low and wide, raises its sights, lowers the visor. The pod splits and the star inside charges, rays turning.
  • The survey. A dashed laser sweeps the floor and drops three stakes. Each gets a crosshair that tightens and blinks faster, with three pips that go out one by one.
  • Star-fire. The pod flies open and the body recoils as columns erupt left to right: white core, cyan, blue, a violet fringe that shivers like a split lens. A freeze on the first, a shake on each. The snow they touch turns to glittering glass.
  • The full bout. Now the survey hunts her: the last stake lands on her spot and she runs between the pillars. Then it scuttles in on six legs and pounces (body slam, after-images, an impact frame); in phase two it throws three stakes round her ledge and triangulates: the lines go live and close in, and collapse into one great pillar. Weak window: the star dims, the pod closes, it slumps.
4 · Return to Sender · violet hellfire, black and crimson

Hollow Courier

A demon who delivers. A floating bell of a coat in black and crimson with brass buttons, a cracked porcelain mask under a courier's cap with a glowing badge, a satchel of sealed letters. Its letters burn violet. It throws one over your head; it hangs behind you, then comes back as lightning.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · draw (the warning) · throw · catch · bow
  • Warning. It rises and leans back with one letter held high behind its head, its other hand pointing. The letter catches violet and the satchel glows.
  • Throw. The arm whips across, the coat flares back, and the letter flies end over end in a high arc over Elfy's head. It stops behind her and hangs there.
  • The tell. While it hangs, its seal pulses faster, a dashed line drops to the floor where the bolt will land, and chevrons crawl along the floor back toward the courier: the bolt's path.
  • Return to sender. The letter bursts into paper ash as lightning strikes straight down, with a flash, a freeze and a shake. Then the bolt runs back along the floor as a low crackling arc, scorching the snow. Jump it. The courier catches it in its glove, and bows.
  • The full bout. Then: a low swoop the length of the room (after-images; she goes up onto the ledge), registered mail (three burning letters hung round her, striking one after another, the last on her ledge), and in phase two it drinks the lightning back in, blinks above her and drops bell-first. Weak window: its satchel bursts and it scrabbles after its letters.
5 · Falling Star · astral prism, midnight velvet and gold

Vesper Maw

A bloated astronomer-priest that clings in the corner by six long legs, three on the ceiling and three on the wall. It wears a midnight robe embroidered with gold constellations, a pleated white ruff and gold bands on its legs, and orrery rings turn round its body. Its head is mostly mouth: needle teeth under a cluster of star-bright eyes. It swallows starlight and spits a falling star.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · charge (the warning) · spit · recover
  • Warning. It pulls back into the corner and the jaw unhinges. Starlight spirals into the mouth, its orrery rings spin faster, the sky dims, a glyph circle turns on the floor under Elfy, and in the last beat a dashed line shows the path the star will fall along.
  • The falling star. Not a burning rock: a prismatic crystal heart that shifts color, wrapped in two turning glyph rings with tiny planets circling it, trailing a tail that runs gold, pink, violet, teal into deep blue and sheds twinkling stars.
  • Impact. A white four-point star flashes across the screen, the longest freeze and hardest shake of any study, prismatic rings, crystal shards and stardust. Then a constellation draws itself above the crater, star by star, and fades. The snow is blasted to dark glass with glowing cracks.
  • The full bout. Then: it crawls along the ceiling and lashes its tongue down onto her (drool and a line warn first); in phase two it roars the sky dark and spits three stars that hang in a constellation whose lines go live, then fall; then it lets go and drops on her ringed spot. Weak window: sprawled on the snow, rings dim, until it leaps back to its corner.
6 · Watchers · eclipse fire, bone and oxblood

Sclera Magus

A tall alien wizard that floats over a turning rune circle, ribbons trailing. Its head is a long pale cranium with one huge vertical eye and a fan of black spines behind it. It wears layered bone robes over an oxblood panel, a black mantle, a sash with an eye on it and a dagger-cut hem. Its fire is an eclipse: black cores, crimson rings, white-hot rims. It sends up a rocket, and what comes out of the burst is looking for you.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · cast (the warning) · launch · command · recover
  • Warning. It lifts both arms and an eclipse orb gathers between its hands, drinking in crimson motes. The great eye dilates and glows, and an eye-shaped reticle closes on Elfy.
  • The rocket. The orb climbs on a firework trail: white-hot behind it, cooling through rose and crimson and breaking up behind, glitter hanging and twinkling along it, the far end crackling.
  • The burst. A firework's streaks and glitter, but for a moment an eye opens in the sky. Five watchers fly out of it on drooping firework trails and hang there, blinking open one by one, pupils turning to Elfy, while the Magus points at her.
  • The dive. One after another they dive on the same crackling trails. Each locks onto where Elfy stands as it starts, steers there for a fifth of a second, then commits to a straight line. Move once they commit and they burst where you stood, in black smoke and crimson sparks.
  • The full bout. Then: it unravels and blinks beside her for an eclipse slash at waist height (she takes the ledge); in phase two its eye opens wide and an eye beam sweeps the floor and the ledge, and the ledge is cover: she crouches under it; then six watchers ring her and tighten until she flips out and they collide. Weak window: eye shut, it sinks into the snow.
7 · Gale · a burning cloud, ash grey and ember red

Grinning Pyrocumulus

A mushroom cloud that grins. A rolling cap of smoke lit red from the fire inside it, a stem feeding it, a condensation skirt, a dust surge at its foot. A wide Cheshire grin full of teeth and two crescent eyes. It drinks in the air, puffs its cheeks, purses its lips and blows, and the wind carries you back into the spikes.

idle
  • The face. The grin is the whole character: a wide Cheshire crescent of teeth over a burning throat, yellow crescent eyes. Drinking the air, the grin shrinks to a pucker, angry brows slant in, the eyes go wide and then white-hot, and the cheeks swell into two huge round puffs. Blowing, the lips purse into a glowing O with pucker wrinkles round it, the eyes scrunch shut, burning red, and the cheeks empty. After, the grin snaps back and it laughs.
  • Warning, in color. At rest it's grey smoke and yellow eyes. As it drinks, the fire climbs up through the cap, the face flushes ember red, the eyes whiten; it holds and trembles for a beat, the flush pulsing, before it blows. The inhale also pulls snow and ash from the whole room into its mouth, and in the last half second chevrons stream toward the pit and the spikes pulse red.
  • The body acts. It rears back and swells a third bigger (anticipation), snaps forward squashed wide the instant it blows, deflates through the gust, and wobbles back on a spring (overshoot and settle). The stem lags behind the cap and bends (follow-through). The ring round the stem circles behind it and in front. The base along the floor ripples like a wave: rolling out at rest, sucked in as it drinks, surging out as it blows.
  • The gale. A hard shake as it starts, then streaks of wind, blown snow and burning ash across the screen and a constant shake. Like Storm Eagle's gust, it doesn't hurt; it moves you. Elfy runs against it and still slides back, faster and faster, toward the edge.
  • The pit. Here she loses: over the edge onto the spikes, a freeze, a white flash and red sparks, and she bounces out blinking onto the far side. Jumping into the wind or holding a wall would save her.
  • The full bout. Then: it drifts over her and rains cinders down three marked lanes; it drinks again but now pulls her in and lunges down to chomp the floor (she escapes to the ledge); in phase two it rises burning and slams, smoke rolling out both ways. Weak window: deflated and panting.
8 · Pitch · gold-magenta plasma, navy hazmat, an ice cave

Ion Gremlin

A tiny imp with absurdly long arms, up on four long spider legs banded with glowing coils, in an ice cave by a cracked containment ring. It wears a puffy navy hazmat parka with reflective stripes and a fur-trimmed hood, its sleeves rolled up its long grey forearms, big brass goggles with plasma for lenses over a needle-toothed grin, and a long striped scarf. Its plasma runs white-hot through electric gold into hot magenta. It balls the plasma up and pitches it, then skitters in for a follow-up.

idle
Key poses, 4×: idle · windmill · windmill · throw (overreach) · lash wind-up · lash (the legs are drawn live)
  • The room. Still winter, but inside: an ice cave of crystal walls, icicles and crystal clusters, a faint aurora caught in the ice that brightens while it charges, and a cracked containment ring half sunk in the snow behind it, its coils glowing, spitting sparks, feeding it.
  • Warning. Its arms windmill overhead faster than you can follow while arcs leap from the ring into its fingertips, the finger claws and goggles go white-hot, a ball of plasma swells above its head, and a target pulses under Elfy.
  • The pitch. One arm whips out to over half again its length, rubber-hose, and the ball flies in a lob, stretching along its arc.
  • The mine. It sticks where it lands and pulses faster, drinking in snow, while a dashed dome shows exactly how far the blast will reach. Get outside the dome.
  • The blast. A white flash, then a ball of plasma swelling into a boiling shell that hollows out, shock rings tilted through it front and back, arcs crackling out into the floor, the longest freeze and hardest shake, glitter, melted snow. Then it cackles, its legs drumming the floor.
  • The legs. Four long spider legs, knees high over its little body, dark chitin banded with glowing magenta coils. They step in a scuttling rhythm as it skitters and drum and tap in a frenzy whenever it's excited, the way its arms windmill.
  • Follow-up: the lash. It skitters in, crouches low on its legs with both arms swung back and crackling, and a dashed line lights across the whole room at head height. Then it cracks a whip of plasma along that line, out to the wall. Crouch: Elfy kneels and it cracks over her head. Then it skitters home to its ring.
  • The full bout. Then: a full-tilt rush into the wall under her ledge, ricocheting out; in phase two it leaps onto the containment ring and drinks it (an impact frame), and pitches three plasma bombs at three marked domes, the last on her ledge, leaving one gap. Weak window: the ring backfires into it; frazzled and seeing stars.
9 · The Lens and the Mortar · a pair: her magic, his fire

Madame Vesper & Little Tubbs

Two who fight as one. Madame Vesper is magic: an alien sorceress taller than anything in the room and thin as a reed, porcelain blue, a long neck and a long face with three rose eyes, black horns swept back and tipped in gold, a stiff fan collar, a narrow gown that bells into a train on the snow, and spider fingers. Little Tubbs is fire: a tiny round stoker wider than he is tall, coal-black skin cracked with embers, mustard overalls with brass buckles, a huge white mustache, a bowler with a stovepipe chimney that smokes, and a furnace door in his belly. She shapes; he burns.

idle
  • Always moving. She sways like a reed, more at the top than the hem, rose motes drifting off her fingers. He bobs on stubby legs, waddles, waves his tiny arms, his stovepipe puffing.
  • The lens. Warning: she points and a rose thread from her finger traces a seal in the air in front of them, runes turning inside it, while he breathes in, cheeks puffed, belly swelling, sparks sucked into his furnace door; a glowing line burns into the snow along the path. Then he spits one little spark through the seal, it flashes, and what comes out the other side is a roaring wheel of fire rolling across the room, melting a trench. Jump.
  • The mortar. Warning: she sweeps both arms up and lifts him in a rose bubble, legs kicking; he curls into a ball and catches fire; a target pulses under Elfy with a dashed dome over it. Then she flings him, and he arcs in, spinning, trailing embers, and slams down: a burst of flame the width of the dome, the hardest shake, snow thrown, a crater that glows and cools. Get outside the dome.
  • The pair. He pops out dizzy with stars round his hat and bounces home in two hops; she catches him on her thread and pats his hat while he laughs smoke rings out of his chimney.
  • The full bout. Phase two, pinball: she draws three seals in the air and curls Tubbs into a fireball, bats him, and he ricochets seal to seal, faster at each, down onto her mark; the path is drawn first. Weak window: he's dizzy and spent, she leans on her fan.

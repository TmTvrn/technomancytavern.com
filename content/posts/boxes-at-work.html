Before Elfy had art, the player was a glowing cyan square, and the whole game feel lived in it. These clips are recorded from the boxes-only build (commit 0f62800), with scripted input so every run is the same. Numbers come from the source and from a log of the player's position every physics tick.

Squash and stretch

Every jump calls visual.punch(Vector2(0.76, 1.26)): the square's visual gets narrower and taller for a moment, then springs back. The collision box never changes; only what you see does. That is the whole trick, and it is why a square with no face can still feel springy.

Wall slide and wall kick

Hold toward a wall in the air and the fall slows to a slide at 150 px/s. Press jump and you kick off it. The log shows the slide speed holding at exactly that number.

Spikes

Spikes are solid like a floor, but touching the points costs a point of health and bounces you away. In the log, health drops from 5 to 4 at the moment of contact and the bounce launches the square over the pit.

Coyote time (and the time I got it wrong)

Coyote time is a short grace period after you walk off a ledge during which a jump still counts as a ground jump. Here it is 0.1 seconds.

My first recording of this looked right and was wrong. I pressed jump just after the square left the ledge and it jumped, so I called it coyote time. Then the question came up: is that just the double jump? It was. The log showed the double jump counter dropping from 1 to 0 at that moment, and the jump was the small air jump, not the full one. My press had landed on the exact tick the window closed.

So I redid it properly: the double jump is switched off for these two runs, and the log confirms the result before I record anything.

Lesson for the devlog, and for anything you record: check the numbers, then the clip. A clip that merely looks right can still be showing something else.

Recorded with Godot's movie mode and encoded with ffmpeg at 960 px wide. The input scripts and the debug patch are in the game repository under docs/devlog/capture/.
